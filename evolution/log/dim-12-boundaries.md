### Round 2 — 2026-06-28 · boundary: Boundary detection by heuristic

- **Change:** refined **dimension 12 (boundary doctrine — method core)**.
  In `dimensions-template.md`: the "What good looks like" field gains the **quadrant
  test** (Diataxis taxonomy — two axes action/cognition × acquisition/application →
  four types: tutorial/how-to/reference/explanation), with the concrete mapping of
  this repo's artifacts (CLAUDE.md=map/reference · `arquitetura/`=active reference ·
  ADR=explanation · backlog=how-to · VISION=explanation of direction) and the
  verifiable criterion "if it serves two purposes, it has two homes"; "Detection"
  and "Smells" now name the **cross-quadrant artifact** (explanation in the
  CLAUDE.md map, reference in an ADR, direction in the architecture). In
  `detection-and-smells.md`: dimension 12 becomes a **3-step routine** (boundary table
  × 3 artifacts · quadrant test per artifact · semantic DRY) + an anchor-directive
  grep (`python define yaml`/`source of truth`/`generated by ci`) that finds
  candidates for duplicated rules. Conceptual diff: dimension 12 stops being just
  "read and compare" and gains a **nameable test and a triage command** — without
  losing the comparative-reading character (the grep only lists candidates, does not
  decide).
- **Why:** dimension 12 is declared the **exclusive value** of the method (the only
  one with no skill owner), but it was the least operational — "there is no grep
  that finds semantic contradiction" left the auditor without a criterion. The repo
  **already has** the boundary doctrine crystallised and consistent in three places
  (root CLAUDE.md; `INDICE.md:24-30` "Boundary rule"; `domain.md:10-11` active vs.
  ADR) — meaning there is a concrete target to verify, and the method was missing
  the lens (quadrant) and the routine to confront it.
- **Sources:** [Diataxis — Foundations](https://diataxis.fr/foundations/) (accessed

  and the four types (tutorial=acquisition/action, how-to=application/action,
  reference=application/cognition, explanation=acquisition/cognition);
  [Docs as Code — Write the Docs](https://www.writethedocs.org/guide/docs-as-code/)
  (accessed 2026-06-28) — DRY/single-source-of-truth as a corollary of versioning
  (each piece of information has one canonical home). Catalogue:
  `research/12-knowledge-architecture-external.md` (cross-quadrant artifact smell;
