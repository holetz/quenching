# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A product owner supervising many coding agents at once, working for hours with the portal beside a terminal. Their job is to see what waits for them, decide it, keep priority, target date and owner current, and get back to the work.

## Product Purpose

The specs portal is a local, keyboard-first cockpit over the specs store of the `quenching` plugin (`cq specs serve`, `cq portal serve`). It exists so one human can keep many agents moving: seeing each spec's stage, approving, unblocking and capturing without reading the store by hand. Success is that what needs a person is obvious at a glance and everything else stays quiet.

## Positioning

A cockpit that reads one store and invents nothing: every number comes from the server, and the stage of a spec is derived, never dragged.

## Operating Context

Runs locally on loopback, opened beside a terminal and used for long sessions. The contract is `docs/standards/architecture/specs-portal.md`; the visual target lives in `design-system/`, with its gap from today's portal in `design-system/refinement.md`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The interface speaks pt-BR; the repository's documentation is English.
- No CDN, no external font, no framework and no build step in the shipped portal.
- `style-src 'self'`: no inline `style` attribute ships, and every node is built with `createElement` and `textContent`.
- No view invents a number the server did not send, and no new write route exists without a human decision.
- `design-system/components/*/preview.html` and `tokens.css` are generated by `design-system/build.py`; lasting changes belong in `build.py`, `components/bundle.css` or `tokens.json`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Quenching.
- Voice: plain and factual, sentence case, no exclamation marks and no emoji. Controls name their action with a verb, and errors name the problem and the remedy.

## Evidence on Hand

Target screens in `design-system/components/Screen*` use invented sample data, never data from a real store. There are no testimonials, customers or benchmarks to cite.

## Product Principles

- The human's attention is the scarce resource: only what waits for them may call for it.
- The keyboard comes first, and every view answers it.
- Show what the server said; a missing number stays missing.
- Widen nothing without a human decision.
