# Brand

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Quiet evidence, unmistakable action."** The system keeps information calm
and legible, then spends one accent on the decision that matters. It should feel deliberate rather
than decorated.

- **Restrained:** hierarchy comes from scale and space before ornament.
- **Evidence-led:** supporting material sits beside the claim it proves.
- **Decisive:** one accent thread identifies action and structure.

## Colors

Use the primary token for action and the editorial thread. Neutral surfaces carry the reading
experience; paired `on-*` tokens govern foreground contrast.

**The One Accent Rule.** One block gets one accent role; a second accent must encode meaning rather
than add variety.

## Typography

Montserrat supplies a direct display voice and a highly legible working voice. Display, body, and
label roles are fixed in the token source and remain consistent between web, Typst, and PDF.

## Layout

Use the spacing scale to create a clear reading rhythm. Long-form text stays within a readable
measure while frames may widen for evidence, diagrams, and comparisons.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth separates functional layers. It never simulates glow or spectacle.

## Shapes

Small radii keep controls precise, medium radii group cards, and the full radius is reserved for
short pills or circular affordances.

## Components

Button, input, navigation, chip, and card are the interoperable base vocabulary. Eyebrow, rule, and
frame extend that vocabulary for editorial artifacts.
