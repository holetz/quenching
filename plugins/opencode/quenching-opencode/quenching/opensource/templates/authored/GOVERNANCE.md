# Governance

## Roles

- **Maintainers** review and merge changes, triage issues, and cut releases: {{maintainers}}.
- **Contributors** are everyone who opens an issue or a pull request.

## Decisions

Day-to-day decisions are made by maintainers through pull request review. A change to this document,
to the license or to the list of maintainers needs the agreement of a majority of the maintainers,
recorded in a pull request.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor with a sustained record of reviewed contributions may be nominated by a maintainer and
is added by the same majority rule.
