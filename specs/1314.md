## Problem

O develop e o conclude fixam `model: opus`, mas em 93/93 runners orquestrados dos últimos 14 dias não houve troca de modelo: sob o `spec-runner` eles rodam no modelo do runner. O manual (`plugins/quenching/README.md`, modelo de custo) promete o contrário.

Toca `agents/orchestrator.md` como a S11; por isso vem depois dela.

Fonte: avaliação do post sobre subagentes (2026-10-08), item S12 da fila `.quenching/autopilot/2026-10-08/round1-queue.md`. Caminhos curtos: `agents/` e `commands/` sob `plugins/quenching/`.

## Proposal

Direção: o manual e o `orchestrator.md` dizem o que acontece de fato; mudar o roteamento está fora.

- O modelo de custo do manual e o `orchestrator.md` dizem que, sob o `spec-runner`, develop e conclude rodam no modelo do runner.

## Out of Scope

- Changing the routing so develop and conclude really run on opus under the `spec-runner` — the Proposal fixes the direction: the docs say what happens; the routing stays.
- The `model: opus` pins in `commands/specs/develop.md` and `commands/specs/conclude.md` — they stay and still apply when a human invokes the command directly.

## Impact

### Standards this spec will write into docs/standards/

- none — a correction of two prose surfaces, no standard is written.

### Product code this spec expects to touch

- `plugins/quenching/README.md` — the cost-model rows of develop and conclude.
- `plugins/quenching/agents/orchestrator.md` — the Route step.
- `plugins/quenching-codex/README.md` and `plugins/quenching-codex/agents/orchestrator.md` — regenerated by `cq components translate --write`.

- `cq components translate --check` exits 0 after the regeneration.
- `bash scripts/verify_repo.sh` exits 0.

## Design

- Durable rule: the `model:` pin of a command is honoured only when a human invokes it directly. Under the `spec-runner` the command runs in the runner's session, on the runner's model (sonnet, or opus when the orchestrator passed `complexity: high`), measured in 93/93 orchestrated runners over 14 days.
- The README rows say it next to the pin; the orchestrator's Route step says it once, so the conductor does not budget opus for develop/conclude.
- Alternative weighed: forcing the switch in the runner. Ruled out by the Proposal (routing is out).

### Mapa de dependências

- `plugins/quenching/README.md` — cost-model rows; the Codex README is its generated translation.
- `plugins/quenching/agents/orchestrator.md` — mirrored into `plugins/quenching-codex/agents/orchestrator.md` by translate.
- `plugins/README.md` and `plugins/quenching-specs-reader/**` — do not carry the cost model; checked after regeneration.

## Alternatives Considered

- Route develop/conclude to a dedicated opus agent: rejected, it changes routing, which the Proposal places out of scope.
- Drop the pins from the two commands: rejected, they still hold on direct human invocation.

## Open Decisions

- none — the Proposal fixes the direction and the wording is a drafting matter.

- The sentence is read as removing the pin: mitigated by stating that direct invocation still honours it.
