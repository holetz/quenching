## Problem

O lint descarta opções ao conferir grants (`_granted_words`, `components/lint.py:264-269`, hoje em `plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/components/commands/lint.py:264`): `cq --root <wt> specs show …` e `git -C <wt> status` contam como cobertos, mas o harness casa por prefixo literal.

Consequência: o lint não enxerga o S6 do `--root`. Depois do S6 (e da S15, que toca o mesmo lint).

Fonte: revisão de correção opus do épico 1289 (2026-10-08), item S8 da fila `.quenching/autopilot/2026-10-08/round1-queue.md`. Reproduções em `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-unicred-Projetos-claude-quenching/a284eb01-1fc3-4a7f-ae95-abf7651b3d1b/scratchpad/review9/` (os caminhos `review9/...` deste texto são relativos ao scratchpad). Caminhos curtos: `agents/` e `commands/` sob `plugins/quenching/`; `git/`, `components/` e `specs/` sob `plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/`.

## Proposal

- `_granted_words` confere a chamada como o harness casa: prefixo literal, com as opções no lugar em que aparecem; `cq --root <wt> specs show …` e `git -C <wt> status` deixam de contar como cobertos por `Bash(cq specs show:*)` e `Bash(git status:*)`.

## Out of Scope

- The negation logic of `extract_calls` (`components/cq_calls.py`): 1315 already changed it on main, and the false-positive and missed-flag cases it covers (`MISS same-clause condition`, `FP wrapped line`, and the rest of `lint/probe.py`) are not this spec's.
- Command bodies' own `allowed-tools` (`_grant_covers` over `_shell_command_lines`): that path already compares the literal command and was not reported.
- Rewriting the agent bodies that carry a displaced option: the lint reports them; if it reports any, each is a `## Discoveries` line, not an edit here.

## Impact

## Design

- **Rule: a grant is matched against the call as written.** The harness matches `Bash(<prefix>:*)` by literal prefix, so the lint joins `binary` and `args` in their order, options included, and asks `_grant_covers` of that string. Options after the verb stay covered (`cq specs show --spec 1` starts with `cq specs show `); options before or between the verbs (`--root`, `-C`) fall outside the prefix and are a gap.
- **Rule: the finding says why.** When the call without its options would be covered, the gap is the displaced option, not a missing grant, so adding the grant would not fix it. The finding then names the option and the remedy is to put it after the verb or `cd` into the worktree first (the steward's own rule). `_granted_words` stays, only to name the `form` and to tell the two causes apart.
- **Rule: dedup per cause.** One finding per `(form, cause)`, so a missing grant and a displaced option on the same verb are both reported.
- Alternative weighed: add `Bash(cq --root:*)` style grants. Rejected: the grant list is per verb and the combinatorics of option positions would defeat the least-privilege reading of `tools:`.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep stripping options and only warn: rejected, the lint would still call covered what the harness refuses, which is the reported defect.
- Parse the grant list into option-tolerant patterns: rejected, the harness does not do that, so the lint would again diverge from it.

## Open Decisions

- none — the Proposal fixes the matching rule; the wording of the message is settled in Design.

## Risks

- A shipped agent body carries a displaced option and becomes a new gate error: mitigated by running `bash scripts/verify_repo.sh` in task 1.1 and fixing the body in that task only if it is inside `files:`, else parked as a discovery.
- Placeholders such as `<wt>` in the call text change the literal: ACCEPTED — they sit after the displaced option anyway and the prefix test fails or passes on the option, not on the placeholder.
