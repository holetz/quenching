## Problem

A revisão de correção do épico 1613 (rodada 17) achou que o registry de ops estrito da #1614 não lê `archived (data, motivo)`, que o `lifecycle.md` exige, nem a declaração em lista, e os arquivados viram `op-orphan`.
Este épico agrupa a correção desse achado (spec 1615) como rodada 18 do autopilot de 2026-10-12.

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 through 1613. After a verifier PASS, the run may execute 1615 forced, push, open the PR and
merge it into `main` with no further confirmation: the conductor sends the leaf to `git-steward` for the merge.
Nothing widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the opus review of epic 1613 reproduced it. The conductor confirmed it against
  `references/ops-align/lifecycle.md:19,34-35`, which requires a reason for archiving.
- 1615 takes the state from the first word of the Lifecycle cell or of the declaration outside the generated zone,
  followed by an optional reason (in parentheses or after a separator), with an optional list bullet before the
  declaration. Free text in the Purpose column is still never read as a lifecycle, and the case fixed by 1612 stays
  covered by its test. A table with no Lifecycle header does not reuse the column index of an earlier table.
