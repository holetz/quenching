## Problem

A revisão de correção do épico 1569 reproduziu uma regressão da #1570 (spec 1568): sem `scripts/verify_repo.sh`, todo move de teste JS/TS falha como `test-deleted`.
A spec 1571 corrige isso restringindo a exigência de destino `.py` ao gate que declara um `unittest discover` reconhecido.

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 through 1569. After a verifier PASS, the run may execute 1571 forced, push, open the PR and
merge it into `main`, with no further confirmation: the conductor sends the leaf to `git-steward` for the merge.
Nothing widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that needs any of these returns
`blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`, and the conductor moves it to human epic 1527.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the opus correctness review of epic 1569 reproduced 1571 against `main` 7ec6bc0b. The map was not run
  again, because every surface that changed since the map that came back empty at 71f8f356 has since been reviewed.
- 1571 restores the fallback by folder name, as before #1570, for any gate that declares no recognized
  `unittest discover`. The `.py` requirement stays only where that discover is recognized. Its regression fixtures
  include a repo with no `scripts/verify_repo.sh` that moves a `.test.js` and a `.test.ts`.
- Not taken as a spec: `--latest-orchestrator` (#1566) picks the wrong orchestrator when an earlier one is resumed
  after a later one has started. The autopilot runs orchestrators one at a time, and all 7 real ones in this session
  resolve correctly. The case is synthetic and touches telemetry only.
