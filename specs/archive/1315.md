## Problem

A negação do lint `sk-agent-grant-gap` vale em qualquer ponto da oração, não só colada à chamada; há falsos positivos de quebra de linha e de "e.g." (sondas em `review9/lint/probe.py`).

## Proposal

**Decidido pelo humano em 2026-10-08.** A negação do lint `sk-agent-grant-gap` passa à adjacência estrita: a palavra de negação a poucas palavras antes da chamada ("never `cq …`", "no `cq …` grant"), não em qualquer ponto da oração; corrigir junto os falsos positivos de quebra de linha e de "e.g." (sondas em `review9/lint/probe.py`).

- Toca `components/commands/lint.py` como a S8; esta vem antes.

## Out of Scope

- The probe's four MISS cases about `--root` / `git -C` prefixes and the option between verbs: they belong to the grant matcher (S8), not to the negation.
- A lint of the corpus wording itself: no body is reworded unless the gate shows the new rule flags it.

## Design

- **Adjacency is a word window.** A negation counts only when it sits within four words before the span, in the same clause. A clause ends at `;`, `:`, `,` or a period followed by whitespace; `e.g.`/`i.e.` and decimals such as `3.1's` never end one.
- **A wrapped sentence is one sentence.** The previous prose line is carried into the window; a blank line or a fence resets it.
- **A negated list is one negation.** Spans joined by commas, `or`, `and` ("never `a`, `b` or `c`") are collapsed so the negation stays adjacent to each. The orchestrator and git-steward bodies depend on it.
- `no` joins the negation words ("no `cq …` grant").

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep the whole-clause negation and only patch the three false positives: rejected, the human decided on strict adjacency.

## Open Decisions

- none — the human decided the design in `## Proposal`.

## Risks

- A real call inside a long negated sentence becomes visible to the lint: ACCEPTED — that is the stricter rule asked for, and the gate shows the corpus stays clean.
- Adding `no` could hide a call after "no longer": ACCEPTED — the window is four words and the case is rare.
