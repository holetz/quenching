## Problem

Na spec 2231 (2026-11-21, develop autônomo), as tarefas 1.1, 2.1 e 2.2 editaram `quenching/assets/bin/quenching/config/schema.py`, `config/__init__.py` e `specs/commands/doctor.py`, mas nenhum `files:` declarou as cópias geradas em `.agents/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/...` nem `.agents/.quenching-deploy.json`. O check `deployed surface lockstep` de `scripts/verify_repo.sh` falhou no execute, e o worker teve de anexar uma tarefa 4.1 (`cq deploy --target . --write`) depois da aprovação — registrado como `decision:` em `## Discoveries` da 2231. A spec 1901 (arquivada) exigiu os espelhos em `files:` desde o develop, mas para as saídas derivadas de antes do 7.0 (espelho Codex, `.generated-files.json`, `plugin.json`); a superfície instalada por `cq deploy` em `.agents/quenching/` não aparece nessa regra. A melhoria deve fazer o develop derivar, para todo caminho sob `quenching/` que o deploy copia, a cópia em `.agents/` (ou uma tarefa de refresh) já no `## Tasks` aprovado.

**Insumos**

- spec 2231, `## Discoveries`: "appended task 4.1 to refresh the deployed surface — the gate's deployed-surface-lockstep check failed because tasks 1.1–2.2 did not declare their generated mirrors"
- AGENTS.md §Operating: "After changing a command body, a reference or the shipped payload, refresh it with `python3 quenching/assets/bin/cq deploy --target . --write`"
- spec 1901 (archive) — a regra anterior para espelhos em `files:`
- fonte a corrigir: `quenching/skills/quenching-specs/references/develop.md` e a referência de mapeamento de tarefas que ele cita; chega ao repositório por `cq deploy`

## Outcome

Abandoned by the autopilot (round 5, 2026-11-21) — the premise is stale. The rule this spec asks for already exists: `quenching/references/specs-develop/artifacts.md:285-289` ("Declare every generated mirror the diff changes") requires `.agents/quenching/**`, `.agents/skills/**`, `.claude/skills/**`, `.opencode/agents/**` and `.agents/.quenching-deploy.json` in `files:`, since 852a7e51a (2026-11-04), and `quenching/references/specs-develop/questions.md:489` repeats it in the gate table. What happened in 2231 was the develop not following an existing rule, and the gate's deployed-surface-lockstep check caught it at execute as designed (the worker appended a refresh task). One occurrence, already caught by the gate: no consequence left that a new spec would remove.
