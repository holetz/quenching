## Problem

Reproduzido pela revisão de correção do épico 1613 (2026-10-12), em `main` db5a7136, e confirmado pelo condutor contra a
referência. A #1614 (spec 1612) passou a ler o lifecycle do registry de ops de forma estrita
(`plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/ops/inventory.py:125-140`): a célula da coluna Lifecycle precisa ser
exatamente `active` ou `archived` (`:134`), e a declaração fora da zona gerada precisa casar
`` `x.py` — active|archived `` sem nada depois (`:139`).

Só que `plugins/quenching/assets/references/ops-align/lifecycle.md:19` manda listar um script arquivado "with the
reason and evidence", e a seção sobre `_archive/` (`:34-35`) exige "a searchable reason for the change". Com
`` `prune.py` — archived (2026-08-30, replaced by run) `` ou com a célula `archived (2026-08-30, replaced by run)`,
o leitor antigo dava `archived`, e o novo dá `None`, e o `ops doctor` passa a reportar `op-orphan`. Uma lista em
marcadores (`` - `run.py` — active ``) também deixou de ser lida.

Consequência: alvos que seguem a referência e anotam o motivo do arquivamento veem os scripts arquivados virarem
`op-orphan` depois do upgrade, e o `/quenching:ops:align` passa a pedir uma declaração que já existe. A correção
aceita o estado como a primeira palavra da célula ou da declaração, seguido de um motivo opcional (entre parênteses
ou depois de um separador), e o marcador de lista opcional. O texto livre do Purpose continua sem ser lido como
lifecycle.

## Proposal

- A Lifecycle cell whose first word is `active` or `archived` is read as that state, with an optional reason after it (in parentheses or after a `—`, `–`, `-`, `:`, `,` or `;` separator), so `archived (2026-08-30, replaced by run)` reads `archived`.
- A declaration outside the generated zone reads the same way, with an optional list bullet before the backticked path: `` - `run.py` — active `` and `` `prune.py` — archived (2026-08-30, replaced by run) ``.
- A table with no Lifecycle header never inherits the column index of an earlier table.
- Free text in the Purpose column, and a Lifecycle cell like `active sessions`, are still never read as a lifecycle.

## Out of Scope

- Changing `references/ops-align/lifecycle.md`: the reference already requires the reason, and the reader is what drifted.
- Parsing the reason into structured data: it is accepted and discarded.
- Any change to the registry writer or to the `op-orphan` check itself.

## Impact

- `plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/ops/inventory.py` (`_registry_lifecycle`).
- `plugins/quenching/tests/test_ops.py`.
- No standards under `/docs/standards/` are written; no generated mirror carries the ops tool.

### Standards this spec will write into docs/standards/

- none — the rule is already stated in `references/ops-align/lifecycle.md`; this restores the reader to it.

## Validation

- `python3 -m unittest discover -s plugins/quenching/tests -p 'test_ops.py'` exits 0.
- The new regression tests fail on the base (`db5a7136`) and pass after the change.
- `bash scripts/verify_repo.sh` exits 0.

## Design

- Decision: a strict state word followed by end-of-cell, or by a separator or an opening parenthesis and then anything. Rule: the state is the first word; what follows is a reason only if introduced by a separator, so `active sessions` stays free text.
- Decision: the column index resets whenever a table row follows a non-table line, so each table must declare its own Lifecycle header.
- Binding contracts: `references/ops-align/lifecycle.md:19,34-35` (reason and evidence on archived entries) and the case fixed by 1612 (`test_purpose_word_does_not_invent_a_lifecycle`, `test_write_then_check_with_active_in_purpose_is_not_stale`) stay green.

## Alternatives Considered

- Making the reference forbid a reason: rejected, the reference requires a searchable reason and evidence for archiving.
- Reading any cell that contains `active` or `archived`: rejected, it is the 1612 regression (Purpose words inventing a lifecycle).

## Open Decisions

- Why now and what would make this not worth doing: the Problem states the regression (archived scripts become `op-orphan` after upgrade); decided by the epic 1616 conductor, who confirmed it against the reference.

- Resetting the column per table could drop a Lifecycle column split across blank lines: ACCEPTED — the registry writer emits one contiguous table.
