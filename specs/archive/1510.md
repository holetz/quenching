## Problem

`plugins/quenching/assets/references/components-command-new/capabilities.md:129-130` (since #1507, spec 1502) says
"a type list in parentheses is ignored in a subagent definition and honoured only for `claude --agent`". But
`plugins/quenching/agents/orchestrator.md:4` declares `tools: Agent(quenching:spec-runner, quenching:verifier, …)`, and
`commands/specs/orchestrate.md:5,41` launches the orchestrator as a subagent. Either the reference is wrong, or the
orchestrator's type list restricts nothing. Decide by evidence (official docs, a measurement on the installed Claude
Code) and correct the reference. If the measurement shows the orchestrator's restriction is not enforced, record it
in `## Outcome` and open a spec in the human epic 1481 (the remedy would touch a grant); do not change `tools:`.

## Proposal

Measured by official evidence: the Claude Code sub-agents page (conferido contra 2.1.296) says the `Agent(type)` allowlist applies only to an agent running as main thread with `claude --agent`; in a subagent definition the type list is ignored. The reference sentence in capabilities.md is therefore correct. Keep it, and add the consequence it implies (an `Agent(...)` list in a subagent grants spawning of any type, so it is documentation, not a restriction). The orchestrator, launched as a subagent by orchestrate.md, is such a case: record in `## Outcome` and open a follow-up spec in epic 1481.

## Out of Scope

- none of the agents' `tools:` change; widening or narrowing a grant is a human decision (epic 1481).
- The nearest lookalike is fixing the orchestrator's restriction; that is the follow-up spec, not this one.

## Impact

- `plugins/quenching/assets/references/components-command-new/capabilities.md` (one sentence in the subagent definition contract).

## Design

A live measurement was not run; the official page is the evidence and the assumption is that the installed 2.1.296 matches it.
