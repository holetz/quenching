## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 and 1534. For every member, after a verifier PASS, the run may execute it forced, push, open the
PR and merge it into `main`, with no further confirmation, leaves included: the conductor sends a leaf to `git-steward`
for the merge. No member widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that needs any of these
returns `blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`, and the conductor moves it to human epic 1527.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the read-only opus correctness review of epic 1534 reproduced every member against `main` a0b44c9a, the
  ref this round starts from. That reproduction stands in for the scout.
- 1540 only tightens the rule. A deletion counts as a move only when the target is a test and keeps its `assert`s.
- 1543 only narrows. It adds a local read (a ref or cache already present, no fetch) to `cq specs status`, and the HUD
  uses it. It adds no network, no hook and no write.
- If 1543's verifier check 9 flags only prose or a `description` change in `plugin.json` or `marketplace.json`, the
  conductor clears it, as it did for 1528. The fail-closed rule itself is spec 1545, with the human.
- Not taken as specs: two side effects of `--move` (#1539). It drops an item's sub-lines, which the standard does not
  define, and leaves an empty wave group. Neither has a concrete consequence.
