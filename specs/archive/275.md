## Problem

On Windows, `test_golden.ProviderGoldenContract.test_every_provider_case_matches_its_frozen_payload` fails for `provider-github-config.json` and `provider-azure-config.json`. It reproduces on the base (`origin/main` `e621dbba4`, in a fresh worktree) with `python -m pytest quenching/tests/test_golden.py`. Found by spec 267 (epic 262), outside the scope of 265, 266 and 267.

**Two causes, measured:**
1. `capture_golden.normalize` replaces `str(workspace)` in `cq`'s stdout. On Windows that stdout is JSON with escaped backslashes (`C:\\Users\\…`), so the workspace is never swapped for `<WS>`. Test-side fix: also replace `json.dumps(str(workspace))[1:-1]`.
2. Once that is normalized, the payload's `path` comes out as `<WS>\.agents\quenching.json` (native, `os.path.join`), while the golden froze `<WS>/.agents/quenching.json`.

**The decision this asks for (a human's):** is a filesystem path inside `cq`'s JSON payload part of the contract in POSIX form (and then the product emits `as_posix()`), or is it native to the platform (and then the golden normalizes the separator after `<WS>`)? No rule in `docs/standards/platform/windows-support.md` settles it.

Files: `quenching/tests/capture_golden.py`, `quenching/tests/test_golden.py`, `quenching/tests/fixtures/golden/provider-*-config.json`, and `quenching/assets/bin/quenching/specs/commands/config*` if the answer is POSIX.

## Proposal

- O `path` que o `cq specs config --json` e o `cq config export|init|check|set --json` emitem fica em forma POSIX em qualquer plataforma (`<WS>/.agents/quenching.json`), pelo mesmo helper.
