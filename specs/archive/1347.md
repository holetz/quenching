## Problem

Regressão latente do lint (1315): com `no` e `skip` (`plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/components/cq_calls.py:34,38`), "When no reviewer answered `cq specs record …`" deixou de ser extraída e "Never skip `cq specs promote …`" já sumia (`/tmp/claude-1000/-home-unicred-Projetos-claude-quenching/a284eb01-1fc3-4a7f-ae95-abf7651b3d1b/scratchpad/review10/lint/surf`).

Origem: item R10 da fila da rodada 2 do autopilot de 2026-10-08 (`.quenching/autopilot/2026-10-08/round2-queue.md`), da revisão de correção opus do épico 1306.

## Proposal

A negação só suprime a chamada que ela de fato nega: `no` modificando outro substantivo ("no reviewer") e a dupla negação ("Never skip") não apagam a chamada `cq` da extração; um teste com as duas frases de `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-unicred-Projetos-claude-quenching/a284eb01-1fc3-4a7f-ae95-abf7651b3d1b/scratchpad/review10/lint/surf`. Depois do R5 (lint).

## Out of Scope

- Reworking the negation model beyond `no` and `skip` (windows, clause ends, listed spans) — only the two words the Problem names are wrong; the rest is covered by existing tests.
- Other double negations (`never fail to run`, `não deixe de`) — no corpus line uses them; a new one is a new spec.

## Impact

### Product code this spec expects to touch

- `plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/components/cq_calls.py` — `NEGATION_RE`, `_negated` and the module docstring
- `plugins/quenching/tests/test_components_lint.py` — one test per vector

## Validation

- `python3 -m unittest discover -s plugins/quenching/tests -p test_components_lint.py` exits 0 with the new tests.
- `extract_calls` over the two phrases of the Problem returns the `cq specs record` and `cq specs promote` calls; `bash scripts/verify_repo.sh` stays at zero errors, so no surface body gains a finding it did not have.

## Design

- Durable rule: a negation hides a call only when it negates it. `no` negates only as the word right before the span (or before `longer`/`more`); `no` before another noun ("no reviewer answered") is a determiner, not a negation.
- Durable rule: a negator followed by `skip` ("never skip", "do not skip") is a double negation that requires the call, so the pair is removed from the clause before the reach window is read. A bare `skips` still negates.
- Alternatives weighed: dropping `no` from `NEGATION_RE` entirely loses "no `cq …` grant"; a word-count heuristic is what already failed. Adjacency is the narrowest rule that keeps both.
- Assumption (low gear, from the repo's tests): existing negation tests (`never`, `do not`, `no … grant`) still hold.

## Alternatives Considered

- Remove `no` and `skip` from the negation words — rejected: reopens the 1315 hole where "no `cq …` grant" and "skips `cq …`" were extracted as calls to run.

## Open Decisions

- none — the two vectors and the guards are settled by the reproduction and the existing tests.

## Risks

- A negation phrase of the form "no longer `cq …`" or "no more `cq …`" must still negate. Mitigation: the adjacency rule admits `longer`/`more`, and a test pins it.
- A previously hidden line becomes visible and the lint reports a finding on a surface body. Mitigation: the gate (`bash scripts/verify_repo.sh`) runs in the last task.
