## Problem

`plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/git/audit.py:182,256` (spec 1470, #1495) stop treating as a CI root any
`plugins/<name>/assets/**/azure-pipelines*.y?ml`, at any depth and in every audited target. Reproduced:
`plugins/foo/assets/deep/x/azure-pipelines-prod.yaml` → SURFACE False, CI_ROOT False. The only file that motivated the
exemption is `plugins/quenching/assets/zensical/azure-pipelines-docs.yml`. Consequence: a real pipeline placed under a
plugin's assets, or that template edited (it is stamped as an adopter's CI by `knowledge/documentation/build.md:70`),
no longer raises `needs-human` in an autonomous run. Narrow the exemption to the one template path. That narrows the
exemption, it does not widen a grant.

## Proposal

- O audit trata como isento apenas o caminho exato `plugins/quenching/assets/zensical/azure-pipelines-docs.yml`.
- Qualquer outro `azure-pipelines*.y?ml` sob `plugins/*/assets/`, em qualquer nome e profundidade, volta a ser SURFACE e CI_ROOT.

## Out of Scope

- none — a mudanca so estreita a isencao; nenhum grant, hook, CI ou allowed-tools e ampliado.

## Impact

### Product code this spec expects to touch

- `plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/git/audit.py` — as regexes SURFACE e CI_ROOT
- `plugins/quenching/tests/test_git.py` — teste da isencao

## Validation

- `python3 -m unittest discover -s plugins/quenching/tests -p test_git.py` passa; o teste novo falha na base.
- `bash scripts/verify_repo.sh` sai 0.

## Design

A isencao vira um lookahead negativo no caminho exato do template (`(?!plugins/quenching/assets/zensical/azure-pipelines-docs\.yml$)`) nas duas regexes, em vez de `plugins/[^/]+/assets/`. Binding: nao ampliar grant; so estreitar.

## Alternatives Considered

- Manter `plugins/[^/]+/assets/` e restringir so a profundidade: rejeitado, ainda isenta nomes arbitrarios.

## Open Decisions

- none — o enunciado fixa o caminho unico.

## Risks

- Outro template legitimo de plugin passaria a exigir revisao humana: ACCEPTED — falha para o lado seguro.
