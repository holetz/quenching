## Problem

A definição da mecânica de pergunta ao humano mora na referência do `/quenching:specs:develop` e nos standards, e ela descreve o formato da ferramenta, não a folha de decisão em texto que o humano pediu em 2026-10-16 (sessão 76eec84e; ver o `## Problem` do épico 1753).

Evidência:

- `plugins/quenching/assets/references/specs-develop/questions.md:57-106` — a mecânica 1 ("Grouped by dependency, and every question carries a recommendation") prescreve um único AskUserQuestion com até quatro perguntas por chamada; L90 "Use **AskUserQuestion** when relevant"; L81-83 a recomendação como opção "(Recommended)". Também L156-195 (§Who answers), L227-229, L268-270, L439 e L498-511 (§Approval).
- `plugins/quenching/assets/references/specs-develop/report-mold.md:220,261` — "then opens an AskUserQuestion".
- `docs/standards/automation/context-discipline.md:404-412` — a recomendação deve viver "inside the question's own payload", nunca em prosa antes da pergunta: conflito direto com a folha, cuja recomendação está no texto de cada Dn.
- `docs/standards/agents/communication.md:144`, `docs/standards/automation/plan-gates.md:42`, `docs/standards/workflows/agent-choice-catalogues.md:94-100` — o mesmo formato de ferramenta.
- `docs/glossary.md` não tem termo para a folha de decisão.
- `plugins/quenching/tests/test_specs_build_cost_contract.py:24-30` confere os marcadores `## Approval — … ## Gathering the evidence` de `questions.md`; eles precisam continuar presentes.

Consequência: enquanto a definição e os standards prescreverem o menu, todo comando que os segue (S2, S3, S4 deste épico) continua emitindo AskUserQuestion, e um standard proíbe exatamente a forma que o humano pediu. Esta spec é a base das outras três.

Restrições fixas (decisões do dono O1–O4, não reabertas aqui):

- O1: a folha em texto é o padrão de toda pergunta ao humano; AskUserQuestion só quando o humano pede um menu.
- O2: nenhum grant de `AskUserQuestion` muda em `allowed-tools` (sk-prose-gate, sk-grant-gap e sk-fork-gate leem o grant como prova do gate). Remover grants está fora de escopo.
- O3: sem teto de 4; todas as decisões independentes numa folha, a dependente na próxima; a folha encerra o turno; resposta ambígua ou incompleta é perguntada de novo, nunca preenchida por omissão; sub-agentes devolvem `STATE: blocked` / `NOTE: needs-human:` com a folha; caminhos autônomos inalterados.
- O4: rótulos da folha na língua declarada (pt-BR aqui).

A forma de referência é a da sessão 76eec84e: linha de abertura com o formato de resposta `D1 A · D2 A · D3 B …`; por decisão `### D<n> — <título>` com contexto, opções `A (recomendada)` / `B` / `C` e consequência de cada uma; dependências declaradas; bloco final "O que corrijo sem pedir, salvo se você vetar". ## Proposal

- `plugins/quenching/assets/references/specs-develop/questions.md` §The four shared mechanics §1 defines the **decision sheet** as the default form of every question put to the human. The definition covers the opening answer-format line (`D1 A · D2 A · D3 B …` plus free corrections), one `### D<n> — <title>` per decision with context, options `A (recommended)` / `B` / `C` and each option's consequence, the declared dependencies, and the closing "what I fix without asking, unless you veto" block. `AskUserQuestion` is used only when the human asks for a menu (O1).
- The same file has no cap of four per call anywhere. Every independent decision goes into one sheet, and a dependent one waits for the next sheet. The sheet ends the turn. A reply that leaves any `Dn` ambiguous or unanswered is asked again, never filled in by default (O3). §Compose, §Discoveries and §Approval say "sheet" where they said "four to a call" or "(Recommended)".
- The sheet's labels follow the declared conversation language (O4). Here that is pt-BR: `D1 A`, `A (recomendada)`, `O que corrijo sem pedir, salvo se você vetar`.
- A sub-agent still never asks. It returns `STATE: blocked` / `NOTE: needs-human:` and carries the sheet as the question. Autonomous paths and the `low` gear do not change (O3).
- `report-mold.md` and the standards `context-discipline.md`, `plan-gates.md`, `agent-choice-catalogues.md` and `communication.md` describe the sheet, not the tool payload. None of them still forbids a recommendation written in the question's own text.
- `docs/glossary.md` carries a **Decision sheet** term pointing at the definition.

## Design

**The decision sheet is the default mechanic, and it is a prose form, not a tool.** `questions.md` §1 keeps its rule about grouping, "a question travels with the ones whose answers cannot change it", and changes the vehicle. The independent questions go into ONE sheet with no cap. The dependent one waits for the next sheet. The sheet's grammar is fixed, from session 76eec84e:

1. an opening line giving the reply format, `D1 A · D2 A · D3 B …`, with free corrections allowed after it;
2. one `### D<n> — <title>` per decision: context ("how it is today", "why"), a table where it helps, and options `**A (recommended):**` / `**B:**` / `**C:**`, each with its consequence. The recommendation is option A, and its reasoning is in A's own text;
3. the dependencies declared: a decision appears early only when it does not depend on an earlier one;
4. a closing block "what I fix without asking, unless you veto": numbered corrections, each with its evidence.

The sheet **ends the turn**, as an explicit stop. A reply that does not resolve every `Dn` without ambiguity is asked again for the unresolved ones and never filled in by default. A free correction in the reply lands like any other answer: it names its section (§4).

**`AskUserQuestion` stays as the menu the human can ask for (O1, O2).** The grant in `allowed-tools` is untouched. The lints read the grant as proof that a gate exists, and the sheet is that same gate in another form. So the definition says when the tool is used ("only when the human asks for a menu"), never that it is gone.

**The labels follow the declared language (O4).** `questions.md` is an English artifact, so it names the parts in English and states that the rendered labels follow the conversation language declared by `communication.md`. For a pt-BR repo that gives `A (recomendada)` and `O que corrijo sem pedir, salvo se você vetar`.

**Sub-agents and autonomous paths do not change (O3).** A sub-agent returns `STATE: blocked` / `NOTE: needs-human:` and carries the sheet. The conductor presents it. `low` still asks nothing.

**The standards follow the definition and do not restate it.** `communication.md` already routes "the mechanics of actually asking" to `questions.md` §The four shared mechanics; its row only names the sheet. `context-discipline.md` §batching moves "inside the question's own payload" to "inside the question itself: option A of each `Dn`", with the ban on a pre-question prose turn kept. `plan-gates.md` and `agent-choice-catalogues.md` replace the tool name with "a decision sheet (or the menu the human asked for)". `report-mold.md` 220/261 say "ends the turn on a decision sheet" and "option A, marked recommended".

**Binding contracts.** O1–O4 in epic 1753 `## Design`. The `test_specs_build_cost_contract.py` markers inside `## Approval —` … `## Gathering the evidence`. `test_specs_context_boundary.py` reads `context-discipline.md` only between `## Run for less time` and `## Measured context budgets` and does not reach the batching paragraph. The Codex mirror is generated, never hand-edited.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Remove the `AskUserQuestion` grants and the tool wording together.** Rejected by O2: the lints read the grant as proof of the gate, and removing it would need lint and CI changes, which this epic forbids.
- **Keep the tool as the default and let the human ask for a sheet.** Rejected by O1: this is the status quo the human rejected in session 76eec84e.
- **Define the sheet in a new standard under `docs/standards/` and leave `questions.md` citing it.** Rejected: `communication.md` already makes `questions.md` §The four shared mechanics the owner of how questions are asked, and a second owner would duplicate the rule.
