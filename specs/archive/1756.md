## Problem

Fora da família specs, os comandos que perguntam ao humano também descrevem a pergunta no formato da ferramenta AskUserQuestion, não na folha de decisão em texto definida pela spec 1754 (S1 do épico 1753; origem no `## Problem` do épico).

Evidência:

- git: `plugins/quenching/commands/git/branch.md:56`, `merge.md:38`, `cleanup.md:34,40` (multiSelect), `revert.md:58`, `push.md:60`, `commit-incremental.md:75`, `pr/create.md:89`
- knowledge: `plugins/quenching/commands/knowledge/align.md:276`
- components: `plugins/quenching/commands/components/command/eval.md:18`, `command/retro.md:44`
- repositório: `.claude/commands/release.md:61`
- testes que fixam o formato: `plugins/quenching/tests/test_git.py:1656-1658`, `plugins/quenching/tests/test_components_lint.py:198`
- notas do espelho Codex: `plugins/quenching/assets/translation/codex-adaptation.json:37` e as notas "four-question gate" (L45, L48)

Consequência: o humano continua recebendo menus nessas famílias, os testes travam o formato antigo e a tradução Codex preserva um "four-question gate" que O3 eliminou.

Restrições fixas (decisões do dono O1–O4, não reabertas aqui):

- O1: a folha em texto é o padrão de toda pergunta ao humano; AskUserQuestion só quando o humano pede um menu.
- O2: nenhum grant de `AskUserQuestion` muda em `allowed-tools`; a entrada `"AskUserQuestion"` de `codex-adaptation.json:37` e qualquer asserção de teste sobre o grant ficam. Remover grants está fora de escopo.
- O3: sem teto de 4; decisões independentes numa folha, a dependente na próxima; a folha encerra o turno; resposta ambígua ou incompleta é perguntada de novo, nunca preenchida por omissão; caminhos autônomos inalterados. A seleção múltipla de `cleanup.md` vira uma decisão da folha que aceita vários itens.
- O4: rótulos da folha na língua declarada.

Depende de S1 (1754). Arquivos disjuntos de S2 e S4. Regenerar o espelho Codex (`scripts/sync_codex_plugin.py`) e passar `bash scripts/verify_repo.sh`.

## Proposal

- Every human question in `git/branch`, `git/merge`, `git/cleanup`, `git/revert`, `git/push`, `git/commit-incremental`, `git/pr/create`, `knowledge/align`, `components/command/eval`, `components/command/retro` and `.claude/commands/release.md` is put as a text decision sheet (shape and rules of `specs-develop/questions.md` §1), not as an AskUserQuestion payload.
- `git/cleanup` expresses its multi-select as one sheet decision that accepts several items, defaulting to none, never to all.
- The tests that pin the old format (`test_git.py` single `**AskUserQuestion**` count) pin the sheet instead.
- The Codex notes in `codex-adaptation.json` no longer preserve a "four-question" gate; the `"AskUserQuestion"` manual-review entry stays.
- The Codex mirror is regenerated and `bash scripts/verify_repo.sh` passes.

- Specs-family bodies (1755), session evidence (1757) and the mechanic itself (1754).

## Design

- **Durable rule:** a command that asks the human says "put a decision sheet (questions.md §1)" and names the decisions, their options and the recommended one A; it does not name AskUserQuestion, which is the menu only when the human asks for one. Owner decisions O1-O4 of epic 1753 are fixed.
- Each rewrite keeps the command's own options and defaults, as sheet options with A the recommended one. Where the old text defaulted to something, that is option A.
- `cleanup` local selection is one decision "which to prune", reply format listing item numbers, `none` default; the remote selection depends on nothing local but stays its own decision on the same sheet when both lists exist; a remote branch is still never folded into the local confirmation (its own `Dn`).
- Single-confirmation commands (revert, push, commit-incremental, pr/create) put one decision: A confirm the exact shown action, B decline. The sheet ends the turn; an ambiguous reply is asked again.
- Sub-agent and `autonomous` branches already return needs-human or ask nothing; wording stays, with the sheet named as the carried content.
- Codex notes: the two "four-question" notes become "preserve its human gate, put as a decision sheet with no cap of four"; the separate test-migration confirmation clause stays.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep AskUserQuestion in these bodies and only change the standards: rejected, it contradicts O1.
- Remove the grants too: rejected by O2.
