## Problem

Achados pela revisão de correção do épico 1576 e pelo mapa da rodada 10 do autopilot de 2026-10-11: o prune remoto com lease falha para sempre em branch já apagado, o proof ratchet grava piso que o próprio --check rejeita, e a sonda de segredos não reconhece padrões .env comuns.
Os três tocam arquivos disjuntos (git/prune.py, proof/ratchet.py, security/probe.py e seus testes) e entram numa só onda paralela.

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 through 1576. For every member, after a verifier PASS, the run may execute it forced, push,
open the PR and merge it into `main` with no further confirmation, leaves included: the conductor sends leaves to
`git-steward` for the merge. No member widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that
needs one returns `blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`, and the conductor moves it to human epic 1527.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the opus review of epic 1576 reproduced 1579. The round-10 map found 1580 and 1581, and the conductor
  confirmed both by reading `ratchet.py:198-260` and `probe.py:95`.
- 1579: a branch already missing on the remote counts as ok and its tracking ref is removed. A rejected lease is
  refused, citing the `--porcelain` reason. The lease itself stays.
- 1580 narrows: the floor is rounded down when raised (or compared with a 1e-6 tolerance), and every root that has
  a floor but no measurement is an error.
- 1581: the ignore globs are normalized, and the probe answers `present` only when every class it asks about is
  covered.
- Not taken as specs: the portal's dropped connection on an unclosed fence (a hand-edited input, nothing written),
  a tag containing a TAB, multi-line lists (no writer produces them), and `proof/ci.py`'s reading of the word
  "pytest" (an unusual CI).
