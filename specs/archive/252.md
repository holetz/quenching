## Problem

Na conclude da spec 1965 (2026-11-23, worktree quenching-slot-2), o `cq deploy --target . --write` respondeu `changes: 0` e o `cq deploy --target . --check` saiu 0. Ao mesmo tempo, quatro espelhos rastreados divergiam da fonte: `.agents/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/opensource/aggregate.py`, `.agents/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/design/doctor.py` e `references/align.md` e `opensource-align/bands.md` de `quenching-opensource` em `.agents/skills/` e `.claude/skills/`. Na main, os quatro batiam byte a byte com a fonte. A seleção de deploy deste checkout é specs, git, knowledge e components, mas `.agents/.quenching-deploy.json` lista os arquivos do opensource. O execute tomou esse `changes: 0` como prova de que os espelhos não eram gerenciados e registrou a decisão de deixá-los velhos. A conclude precisou copiar à mão e rodar o deploy de novo, e aí o manifesto foi atualizado. A melhoria tem de impedir que um espelho rastreado e listado no manifesto fique velho com `--check` verde.

Correção mínima proposta: `cq deploy --check` falha quando um arquivo listado no manifesto diverge da fonte, mesmo que o módulo esteja fora da seleção.

## Proposal

- `cq deploy --check` exits 1 when a tracked mirror of a module outside the selection (skill folder under `.agents/skills/<name>/` or package under `.agents/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/<pkg>/`) differs from the source.
- `cq deploy --write` refreshes those mirrors from the source and records the mirrored folders in the manifest (`mirrors`), so the next `--check` reads them again.
- A module the target never carried stays absent; a module the caller deselects still leaves with the write (narrowing is unchanged).

## Out of Scope

- Installing a module outside the selection that the target does not carry — the selection stays the only way to install.
- Auditing the `deploy check/write` contract as a whole — the follow-up spec 472 owns it.

## Impact

### Standards this spec will write into docs/standards/

- `docs/standards/architecture/deploy-mode.md` — the check/write contract for mirrors outside the selection

### Product code this spec expects to touch

- `python3 quenching/assets/bin/cq deploy --target . --check` exits 1 on this checkout before the refresh and 0 after `--write`.

## Design

- **Where it lives.** The deploy tree builder, after the selected modules: for each module outside the selection, a skill folder (with its `SKILL.md`) or a package (with a `.py`) already present in the target is built from the source like a selected one and returned as `mirrored`.
- **Narrowing stays.** A folder the previous manifest owns is a deselected module and leaves with the write, unless the manifest records it under `mirrors`.
- **An unowned stale mirror is `update`, never a conflict.** The source is the truth for a tracked mirror.
- **Alternative rejected:** compare against a whole `--modules all` tree — drags hooks and assets of modules the target does not use into the check.

### Mapa de dependências

(varredura inline: executor sem Task, 2026-11-25) `deploy/tree.py` and `deploy/cli.py` are used only by `cq deploy` and the deploy tests; the deployed mirrors under `.agents/` regenerate from them.

### Assumptions

- A skill folder without `SKILL.md`, or a package with no `.py`, is target residue, not a mirror — Design.

## Alternatives Considered

- Fail `--check` only, leaving `--write` unchanged — rejected: `--write` would keep reporting `changes: 0` and the red could not be cleared by the tool.

## Open Decisions

## Risks

- First `--check` after upgrading reports `adopt` for mirrors the manifest never owned — ACCEPTED: one `--write` records them.
- A module deselected and re-run with leftover owned files is not mistaken for a mirror — mitigated by the narrowing tests.
