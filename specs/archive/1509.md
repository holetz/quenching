## Problem

Since #1505 (spec 1499), `TASK_META_RE` in `plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/specs/parse/tasks.py:19` (and its copy
in `plugins/quenching-specs-reader`) accepts a `- ` bullet. Together with `re.IGNORECASE` and "last one wins" (lines
135-147), a prose bullet under a task overrides the real metadata. Reproduced: with `      verify: make t` plus
`  - Verify: run it by hand in the browser`, the base gives `verify: "make t"`, main gives `"run it by hand in the browser"`.
`  - Files: also touch whatever the linter needs` replaces `["a.ts"]`. Consequence: a note becomes the shell command of
the verify loop, and the real verification disappears; the real `files:` leaves the verifier's scope check (a false
FAIL). Latent today: only archive 1297 and 1470 use bullets, legitimately. The bare form must win over the bullet form,
and the bullet form must not be case-insensitive.

## Proposal

- A bare metadata line (`      verify: x`, `      files: a`) wins over a bullet-form line of the same key under the same task, whatever the order.
- The bullet form (`  - files:`) matches only the lowercase key; `  - Verify: …` / `  - Files: …` prose bullets are ignored as metadata.
- The bare form stays case-insensitive and the legitimate lowercase bullet form (archive 1297 and 1470) keeps parsing.

## Out of Scope

- Removing bullet support altogether: spec 1499 added it on purpose, and archived specs use it.
- Making the bare form case-sensitive: not part of the reproduced failure.
- Rewriting how `last one wins` resolves repeated bare lines.

## Design

Decision: the metadata loop records which keys appeared in the bare form for the current task. A bullet-form match is skipped when its key was already seen bare, and when its key text is not exactly lowercase. A later bare line still overrides an earlier bullet one, so bare always wins. The two parser copies stay identical.

Reproduced on main: `      verify: make t` plus `  - Verify: run it by hand` yields the prose as verify; `  - Files: also touch…` replaces `["a.ts"]`.
