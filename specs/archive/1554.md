## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522, 1534 and 1546. For every member, after a verifier PASS, the run may execute it forced, push,
open the PR and merge it into `main`, with no further confirmation, leaves included: the conductor sends leaves to
`git-steward` for the merge. No member widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that
needs one returns `blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`, and the conductor moves it to human epic 1527.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the opus correctness review of epic 1546 reproduced every member against `main` 06d9ef79, the ref this
  round starts from.
- 1551 tightens only. Every regression fixture must also change an unrelated code file that sorts before the
  deleted test, so the order-dependence of `added` stays covered.
- 1552 narrows only. `--no-fetch` resolves no remote, calls no API and writes no cache on either backend. A missing
  local ref is reported as such.
- 1553 is prose and comments. ADRs keep their decision and gain a pointer or a note, never a rewrite.
- 1552 and 1553 both regenerate `plugins/quenching-specs-reader`. The one merged second regenerates it again on the
  base during its conclude.
