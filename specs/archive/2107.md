## Problem

Em 2026-10-25, o verifier da spec 63 do bricksmith foi lançado a partir da sessão do quenching e não conseguiu ler o estado da spec, porque `cq specs status` e `cq specs show` não aceitam `--root` no nível do verbo. A 2106 deu `--root` aos verbos de leitura do `cq git`, e falta o mesmo contrato para os verbos de leitura de `cq specs` que o verifier usa (`status`, `show`, `section`). O `cq specs --root` global existe, mas os grants do verifier são `Bash(cq specs status:*)` e `Bash(cq specs show:*)`, e uma flag antes do verbo não casa com esses padrões.

Done when: `cq specs status|show|section --root PATH` funcionam, com teste a partir de outro cwd; o verifier e o orchestrator citam o uso em runs entre repositórios; espelhos regenerados; gate verde. Nenhum grant muda.

## Proposal

- `cq specs status|show|section --root PATH` parses at verb level and reads the repository at PATH from any cwd.
- The global `cq specs --root PATH` keeps working, and a verb-level `--root` wins when both are given.
- Without either `--root`, the cwd still decides, exactly as before.
- The verifier and orchestrator prompts cite `cq specs status|show --root <repo>` for runs between repositories.
- The generated mirrors are regenerated and the repository gate is green; no grant changes.

## Out of Scope

- `--root` on the other `cq specs` verbs: only the three read verbs the verifier uses are in the problem; widening is a separate decision.
- Any change to `tools:` grants, hooks, scripts or CI: `Bash(cq specs status:*)` already matches a flag after the verb.
- Spec 2108 and the `cq git` verbs (done by 2106).

## Design

- Decision: add `--root` to the three verbs with `default=argparse.SUPPRESS` and the same `dest` as the global option. A plain default of None would overwrite a global `--root` parsed first; SUPPRESS leaves it intact and makes the verb-level value win when present. Rule: a verb-level option sharing a dest with a global one never carries a default.
- Resolution stays in `main` (`find_repo_root(abspath(args.root or cwd))`), so no verb changes.
- Binding contract: the verifier's grants are `Bash(cq specs status:*)` and `Bash(cq specs show:*)`; a flag after the verb matches them, a flag before it does not.
- Mirrors are generated, never hand-edited: run `python3 scripts/generate_plugin_modules.py` and the sync scripts, then declare exactly what changed.

### Mapa de dependências

- `specs/commands/cli.py` is copied verbatim into six plugin trees (quenching-specs, -specs-reader, -codex, -opencode, -omnigent); the generators own them.
- `agents/verifier.md` and `agents/orchestrator.md` fan out to quenching-specs, codex, opencode and omnigent copies plus `.generated-files.json` manifests.
- Nothing else reads the option; `specs/mcp.py` already passes the global `--root`.

## Alternatives Considered

- Document the global `cq specs --root` and add a grant `Bash(cq specs --root:*)`: rejected, it changes a grant and needs a human.
- Make the verifier `cd` into the repo: rejected, no grant matches a `cd <repo> &&` prefix.

## Open Decisions

## Risks

- A verb-level default would shadow the global `--root` silently; mitigated by SUPPRESS and a test passing both.
- A mirror missed in `files:` becomes an out-of-scope diff; mitigated by running the generators and reading the diff before the task is checked.
