## Problem

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522, 1534, 1546 and 1554. For every member, after a verifier PASS, the run may execute it forced,
push, open the PR and merge it into `main`, with no further confirmation, leaves included: the conductor sends leaves
to `git-steward` for the merge. No member widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that
needs one returns `blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`, and the conductor moves it to human epic 1527.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the opus correctness review of epic 1554 reproduced both members against `main` 71f8f356, the ref this
  round starts from. This round's map (3 scouts, every area) found no other candidate.
- 1558 corrects the measurement of an audit the human decided (spec 1517). It does not redesign it. The audit and the
  report's cost cover only the orchestrator's descendants, found through `parentAgentId`. The `Agent(...)` list and
  the human's decision on it stay as they are.
- 1559 tightens the moved-test pairing. A test whose target leaves the directory the gate discovers is reported. A
  rename inside it with the same content is accepted. Pairing is no longer greedy. Asserts in docstrings or strings
  and an `exit 0` prefixed to the gate are out of scope: they are deliberate evasion, and the audit is no sandbox.
