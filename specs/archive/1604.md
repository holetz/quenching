## Problem

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 through 1601. After a verifier PASS, the run may execute 1603 forced, push, open the PR and
merge it into `main` with no further confirmation: the conductor sends the leaf to `git-steward` for the merge.
Nothing widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that needs any of these returns
`blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the opus review of epic 1601 reproduced 1603 against `main` 9bcb5ef6, comparing it with a9217ed7.
- 1603 splits the skip set in two. Environments and caches (`.venv`, `venv`, `.tox`, `__pycache__`, `node_modules`)
  are pruned at any depth. Build, dist and vendor are pruned only at the top level of the surface walk; the test walk
  keeps its current behavior. Config files are read without interpolation (`interpolation=None`), and `.pytest.ini`
  enters pytest's order. Still no `.gitignore` reader and no subprocess.
- Not taken as a spec: a hidden test folder (`tests/.hidden/`). The report is ambiguous about pytest's collection,
  and the behavior predates this work.
