## Problem

O autopilot de 2026-10-11 deixou cinco decisões do humano sobre o protocolo autônomo: a allowlist `Agent(...)` do orchestrator é só documental num subagente (1517), o verifier não confere correção (1518) nem régua rebaixada no diff (1519), a escalada do segundo FAIL não recomenda destino (1520) e o front não tem painel de estado barato (1521). Este épico ordena as cinco specs, que compartilham `orchestrator.md`, `verifier.md` e as contagens de agentes, em três ondas para que nenhuma reescreva a linha que outra acabou de mudar.

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`. For every member, after
a verifier PASS, the run may execute it forced, push, open the PR and merge it into `main`, with no further
confirmation. That includes the leaves: the orchestrator leaves their PRs open, and the conductor sends them to
`git-steward` for the merge step under this same policy.

The only widenings allowed are the ones each member's `## Problem` records as a dated human decision (2026-10-11):
1518 may add `quenching:reviewer` to `orchestrator.md`'s `Agent(...)` list and create a read-only opus agent whose
Bash serves only to reproduce; 1521 may add the `quenching-hud` plugin to the marketplace and a validation step to
`scripts/verify_repo.sh`. Any other widening of a grant, of a hook, or of a surface that executes code goes to the
run's human epic and is not taken.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- All five members were proven relevant against `main` 153abe65 by one scout. None of their gaps is closed.
- The waves serialize shared files: `orchestrator.md` (1517 → 1520 → 1518), `verifier.md` (1517 → 1519), and the
  agent counts in the README and docs (1521 → 1518).
- 1517 leaves the location of the agent-type audit to develop, as the human's decision delegates. The conductor
  accepts any of the three locations the decision names.
