## Problem

`claude plugin test integrations/claude-cache` (check "quenching-cache tests" de `scripts/verify_repo.sh:233`) falha de forma intermitente em `tests/bar.test.tsx` com `Error: timed out after 5000 ms`.

- Reprodução em origin/main (9568ac5ae), worktree base detached: 2 rodagens seguidas falharam em "ticks to yellow, then red, with exactly one pop-up". Na worktree da 1985 (que não toca `integrations/`), três rodagens falharam em testes diferentes cada vez ("a closed bar also stops the pop-up", "subagent steps do not move the clock", etc.).
- Os testes dependem de relógio real e estouram o timeout padrão de 5 s; a causa exata (relógio real vs. carga, timeout, tempo simulado) não foi diagnosticada.
- Efeito: o gate local sai 1 em toda sessão, mesmo com `env -u OPENCODE_SESSION_ID`.
- Arquivos: `integrations/claude-cache/tests/bar.test.tsx`.

## Proposal

- `claude plugin test integrations/claude-cache` passes deterministically under machine load: no test in `tests/bar.test.tsx` gets close to the kit's 5 s test timeout.
- Every behaviour the file asserted before is still asserted: the bars' text, the single pop-up, the closed bar silencing it, the subagent clock, the model-switch and compact resets.

## Out of Scope

- Changing the plugin's hooks (`hooks/register.tsx`): its 1 s ticker is the product's behaviour, not the defect.
- Skipping, `xfail`ing or removing the "quenching-cache tests" check in `scripts/verify_repo.sh`, or any test: refused by the epic's decision.
- `handoff.test.ts` and `logic.test.ts`: they never approach the timeout.

- Every test name in the file that asserted a behaviour is still present.

## Design

- **Cause (diagnosed).** The plugin redraws through `$.clock.every(1000, …)`, and the kit's `clock.advance(ms)` fires that ticker once per simulated second, each tick doing async store reads/writes. A 57–80 simulated-minute advance is 3,400–4,800 ticks: about 1.6 s idle, over 5 s when the host is busy. The kit's `test()` takes no timeout argument (a third argument fails the file load) and `clock.now` cannot be re-registered, so the only lever is fewer simulated seconds.
- **Rule.** A test that only reads the bar after a long wait starts from a saved record of that age (the store-seeded `saved(age)` helper); a test about what a tick does advances only the minutes that matter (the 1 s ticker still crosses the expiry boundary once). A cache that expires can also be a 5 min write (`ttl: 5`) instead of a 60 min one.
- **Contract.** No assertion is weakened: expected strings stay the same where the scenario is the same; where the wait shrank, the expected remaining minutes follow the arithmetic (e.g. 5 min advance → 55 min left).

## Alternatives Considered

- Raising the timeout: not available in the kit (a third `test()` argument breaks the file load).
- Overriding `clock.now` to skip time without ticks: refused by the kit (`on("clock.now") registered twice`).
- Dropping or skipping the long tests: forbidden by the epic.

- The kit may later add a timeout or time-jump API; this fix needs no change then.
