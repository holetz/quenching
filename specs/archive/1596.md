## Problem

Os três tocam arquivos disjuntos (delivery/inventory.py, security/probe.py, proof/inventory.py e seus testes) e entram numa só onda paralela.

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 through 1590. For every member, after a verifier PASS, the run may execute it forced, push,
open the PR and merge it into `main` with no further confirmation, leaves included: the conductor sends leaves to
`git-steward` for the merge. No member widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that
needs one returns `blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`, and the conductor moves it to human epic 1527.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the opus review of epic 1590 reproduced 1594 and 1595 against `main` a845eef0.
- 1594 counts `pr:` (when it is not `none`) and `schedules:` as Azure triggers, and `schedule` inside `on` on GitHub.
- 1595 strips the `GIT_*` variables from the probe subprocess's environment and adds the literal names that carry a
  path to the names tested. No new call.
- 1589 (carried over from epic 1590) takes the narrow path its Problem already allowed. Its task 1.1 (ab92ddce, pytest
  discovery order plus skipping dependency and build folders) stays. Its task 1.2 (9c6c59b1, a partial pure-Python
  `.gitignore` reader) is undone with a `git revert` commit in the same worktree, never a reset. Proposal, Design,
  Discoveries and Outcome are rewritten to the shipped path: no reader, and no subprocess in the proof front. The
  subprocess route would widen an executable surface and is not taken.
