## Problem

`plugins/quenching-codex/.generated-from.json` guarda um único `source_sha256` da árvore inteira de `plugins/quenching`, e `.generated-files.json` um sha256 por arquivo; `cq components translate --write` regrava os dois em toda spec que toca `plugins/quenching/**`. Duas PRs paralelas mudam a mesma linha do hash agregado, então a segunda sempre conflita.

Medido nos logs de sessão do épico 1163 (últimos 14 dias): as spec-runners de conserto de conflito da 1164, 1165 e 1166 (348K tokens) conflitaram só nesses dois arquivos, 3 das 11 specs da onda paralela; o verifier reprovou 4 de 25 vezes, a 1164 duas por CONFLICTING/DIRTY; o merge do #1174 exigiu intervenção manual da sessão condutora.

Consequência: cada onda paralela paga um worker e um verifier extras por PR tardia, e o pouso do épico serializa à mão.

## Proposal

Tirar dos arquivos versionados o que muda com qualquer edição não relacionada (o hash agregado da fonte) e deixar o frescor a cargo de `translate --check`, que já regenera e compara; ou tornar o manifesto mesclável por construção (uma linha por arquivo, sem agregado). Conferir antes que `scripts/validate_codex_plugin.py`, o lockstep e o `/release` não dependem do agregado.

## Out of Scope

O layout do pacote Codex e o conteúdo traduzido.

### Product code this spec expects to touch

- the generated mirrors (`plugins/quenching-codex/**`, `.agents/.generated-from.json`, `plugins/quenching-specs-reader/.generated-from.json`)

## Validation

- `python3 -m unittest discover -s plugins/quenching/tests -p test_components_translate.py` exits 0, including a new test: a source edit changes `.generated-files.json` only on the edited file's own line and leaves `.generated-from.json` byte-identical.
- `python3 plugins/quenching/assets/bin/cq --root . components translate --check --json` exits 0, and the same command exits non-zero when one command body is edited without regenerating (stale translation still reproves).
- `python3 scripts/sync_specs_reader_plugin.py --check` exits 0.
- `grep -rn source_sha256 plugins/quenching-codex/.generated-from.json plugins/quenching-specs-reader/.generated-from.json .agents/.generated-from.json` prints nothing.
- `bash scripts/verify_repo.sh` exits 0.
## Design

**Rule: a versioned generated manifest carries no value that changes with an unrelated edit.** The aggregate `source_sha256` is such a value; it is removed from the three `.generated-from.json` files. Evidence: `validate_codex_plugin.py` reads only `command_count`; `/release` and the lockstep read `VERSION`/`plugin.json`, never the digest (grep over scripts/, tests/, references/ and commands/ found no other reader).

**Freshness proof is unchanged in strength.** `translate --check` and `sync_specs_reader_plugin.py --check` regenerate and compare bytes; the digest was never what gated staleness, only what `reconciliation` read. `reconciliation` now derives source-changed from the regenerated tree versus the recorded per-file sha256 map (`.generated-files.json`), and target-changed from disk versus that map, which yields the same four outcomes (untracked, in-sync, source-changed, target-changed, both-changed).

**`.generated-files.json` keeps one line per file** (`"path": "sha"`), so two branches editing different commands touch different lines and merge. The `translate --write` payload may still report a computed digest in its stdout; it is not versioned.

## Alternatives Considered

- Keep the digest and make it per-directory: still conflicts whenever two PRs touch the same directory (all commands share one). Rejected.
- Move the digest out of git into CI only: loses the offline-readable stamp for no gain, since `--check` already proves freshness. Rejected.

## Open Decisions

- none — the only judgement call (adjacent per-file lines may still conflict when two PRs edit alphabetically adjacent commands) is accepted under Risks.

## Risks

- A consumer reads `source_sha256` as the freshness proof: grep found none; `--check` stays the proof. Mitigated by the stale-translation validation above.
- Adjacent per-file hash lines can still conflict in git: ACCEPTED — rarer by orders of magnitude than the single shared aggregate line, and the real conflict surface (the same file) conflicts regardless.
