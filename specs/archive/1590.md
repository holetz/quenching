## Problem

Achados pela revisão de correção do épico 1582 e pelo mapa da rodada 11 do autopilot de 2026-10-11: a sonda de segredos compara texto em vez da semântica do .gitignore, o toolchain doctor acusa dependência dev fixada como não fixada, o delivery doctor lê gatilho em lista como ausente e trigger: none como gatilho, e o proof doctor lê o pyproject no lugar do pytest.ini e conta node_modules como superfície não medida.
Os quatro tocam arquivos disjuntos (security/probe.py, toolchain/inventory.py e checks.py, delivery/inventory.py e checks.py, proof/inventory.py e seus testes); com no máximo 3 workers simultâneos, três entram na onda 1 e o delivery entra na onda 2.

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 through 1582. For every member, after a verifier PASS, the run may execute it forced, push,
open the PR and merge it into `main` with no further confirmation, leaves included: the conductor sends leaves to
`git-steward` for the merge. No member widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that
needs one returns `blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`, and the conductor moves it to human epic 1527.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the opus review of epic 1582 reproduced 1586 with git as the oracle. The conductor reproduced 1587,
  1588 and 1589 by running `cq <front> doctor --json` in throwaway repos.
- 1586 uses git's own ignore semantics (`git check-ignore --no-index` on a representative name for each class)
  instead of comparing text. Its regression tests use git as the oracle.
- 1587 separates the name and extras from the specifier, and accepts npm's bare exact version.
- 1588 accepts list-form triggers and treats `trigger: none` as no trigger.
- 1589 follows pytest's config discovery order and stops counting dependency or build folders as surfaces. The
  preference is to count only what git tracks.
- 1588 waits for 1587 only to keep at most 3 workers running. They share no file.
