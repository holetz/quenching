## Problem

O mapa da rodada 17 do autopilot de 2026-10-12, na fonte de ops, achou que `_registry_lifecycle` (`ops/inventory.py`) lê o lifecycle da linha inteira do registry, e um Purpose com "active" ou "archived" faz o `--check` acusar stale logo após o `--write`.
Este épico agrupa a correção desse achado (spec 1612) como rodada 17 do autopilot de 2026-10-12.

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 through 1610. After a verifier PASS, the run may execute 1612 forced, push, open the PR and
merge it into `main` with no further confirmation: the conductor sends the leaf to `git-steward` for the merge.
Nothing widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the round-17 map found it in `ops/inventory.py:116-126`, and the conductor confirmed it by reading it
  against `ops/registry.py:40-47`.
- 1612 reads the lifecycle only from the lifecycle column of a table row (or from an explicit declaration outside the
  generated zone), never from the whole line. The regression test runs `--write` then `--check` on a purpose that
  contains "active" and expects no `stale`.
- Not taken as specs, from the same map: `lstrip("./")` on hidden paths, two generated zones, a syntax error in a
  script, and `.venv` under `opsRoot`. Each needs a contrived input or a hand-corrupted registry.
- Not taken from the review of epic 1610: exit 0 on `--raise` with no findings (an unambiguous success, already
  reported as the "ratchet result" by `align.md:121`), and the "new roots only" wording (imprecise, but it leads to
  no wrong action).
