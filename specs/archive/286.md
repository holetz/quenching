## Problem

Visto em 2026-11-23 pelo orquestrador do épico 262: a 268 fez o check 4 do spec-verifier tratar como implícitos os arquivos que o deploy regenera de uma fonte alterada. Mesmo assim, `execution.md` (linha 236) e `artifacts.md` (linha 299) ainda mandam o executor declarar esses espelhos e o manifesto em `files:`. Os workers seguem a referência e incham o `files:` (e o `estimate`) com arquivos que ninguém lê. A correção é cortar as duas instruções, o que também economiza tokens nas leituras.

## Proposal

- `execution.md` stops telling the executor to declare generator outputs (mirrors, manifests) in `files:`; it states the 268 rule instead: the verifier's check 4 treats deploy-regenerated paths as implicit.
- `artifacts.md` drops the paragraph `Declare every generated mirror the diff changes` and says in one line that a task declares only the source it edits.

## Out of Scope

- `spec-verifier.md` check 4 itself (the 268 rule stays as it is).
- Translating the references (spec 237 owns that).

## Impact

### Process surface this spec expects to touch

- `quenching/references/specs-execute/execution.md` - the generator-outputs paragraph
- `quenching/references/specs-develop/artifacts.md` - the generated-mirror paragraph

## Validation

- `bash scripts/verify_repo.sh` deployed-surface lockstep passes after `cq deploy --write`; on Windows the platform-red is inconclusive and CI is the proof.
- `grep -n 'in .files:.' ` on both references finds no instruction to declare mirrors or manifests.
## Design

Check 4 of `spec-verifier.md` already removes the payload's `generated` copies and every path that is a key of the branch's deploy manifest when `changed` holds a path under `quenching/`. Declaring those in `files:` is redundant and inflates `files:` and the estimate. The edit cuts the two instructions and keeps the one fact worth keeping: a hand-edited generated file is still out of scope.

## Alternatives Considered

- Leave the text and relax the verifier: rejected, 268 already settled the verifier.

## Open Decisions

- none - the epic Design records 286 as owner of both files.

## Risks

- A worker reads the shorter text as permission to hand-edit a mirror. Mitigation: the kept sentence says a hand-edited generated file still fails scope.

## Outcome
