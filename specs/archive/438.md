## Problem

Na spec 430 (2026-11-24, develop autônomo dentro do `quenching-spec-runner` do OpenCode), o passo 3c de `/quenching-specs develop` manda "dispatch one read-only `Task` sub-agent under the wider profile of §Gathering the evidence" para a varredura de dependências quando `## Design` não tem `### Mapa de dependências`. O agente que roda esse develop declara `tools: task: false` (`.opencode/agents/quenching-spec-runner.md:9-10`), então o passo não pode ser seguido como escrito; o worker improvisou compondo o mapa ele mesmo e anotou isso no mapa. O fluxo não diz o que fazer quando o executor não tem `Task`, e o resultado (mapa composto pelo próprio worker, sem o perfil "wider") passa sem nenhum sinal.

Minimal fix proposed: o passo 3c declara a alternativa sem `Task` (o próprio executor compõe o mapa sob o mesmo perfil e a linha do mapa diz que foi assim), ou o perfil do spec-runner ganha um `Task` só de leitura.

**Insumos**

- `quenching/skills/quenching-specs/references/develop.md` §3c (fonte; chega ao repositório por `cq deploy`)
- `quenching/references/specs-develop/questions.md` §Gathering the evidence (o perfil "wider")
- a definição do agente que gera `.opencode/agents/quenching-spec-runner.md` (`tools: task: false`)
- spec 430, `## Design` › `### Mapa de dependências`: "composto nesta passada a partir da leitura da árvore (sem sub-agente: o worker não tem `Task`)"

## Proposal

- `/quenching-specs develop` §3c names what the sweep does when the executor has no `Task` tool: the executor runs the same sweep itself, inline, under the same wider (read-only) profile, and writes nothing but the table.
- The map written that way carries a marker on its dated line saying the sweep ran inline without a sub-agent, so a reader can tell it from a sub-agent's map.
- `questions.md` §Gathering the evidence and the dependency-sweep standard state the same alternative, so the three sources agree.
- No agent grant changes: the OpenCode spec-runner keeps `task: false`.

## Out of Scope

- Giving the OpenCode `quenching-spec-runner` a read-only `Task` — a widening of an agent's tools, ruled out by the grant rule (grants are only ever narrowed).
- The optional narrower-profile readings of compose/refine: they are already "optional and delegable"; without `Task` they are simply read inline, which the existing text allows.
- Any change to `quenching/assets/bin/quenching/mcp/` (spec 441).

## Design

**Rule.** Where the executor has no `Task` tool (a sub-agent profile declaring `task: false`), §3c's sweep is not skipped and not dispatched: the executor performs it inline with the read-only tools it holds, produces the same one table, and holds it for step 7's single write. The map's date line says `(varredura inline: executor sem Task)`. The evidence for "no `Task`" is the executor's own tool list, never a guess. The wider profile stays the definition of what the sweep may read; inline, the executor writes nothing but the table.

**Why a marker.** The map is trusted by later passes ("swept at most once"); an inline map has not had a cold-context reader, so the marker keeps the difference visible instead of silent (the failure in the spec 430 run).

## Alternatives Considered

- **Give the spec-runner a read-only `Task`.** Rejected: widens an agent's tools; the grant rule forbids it, and the flow can be fixed on the body side.
- **Skip the sweep when there is no `Task`.** Rejected: loses the dependency data the sweep exists to put on the table before the pass asks anything.

## Open Decisions

- none — the fallback, its marker and the three places it is stated are decided above.

## Risks

- An inline sweep reads in the executor's own context and costs more of it than a sub-agent would. ACCEPTED — the only alternatives are skipping the sweep or widening a grant.
- Source and mirrors drift. Mitigated by redeploying in the task and by the gate's deployed-surface lockstep check.
