## Problem

A revisão de correção do épico 1522 (2026-10-11) reproduziu achados no HUD, no lockstep de release, no detector de régua rebaixada, no gate e em `cq specs epic`.
Este épico agrupa as cinco specs soltas que os corrigem (1528–1532), em ondas que serializam os membros que tocam o mesmo arquivo.

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epic 1522. For every member, after a verifier PASS, the run may execute it forced, push, open the PR and
merge it into `main`, with no further confirmation, leaves included: the conductor sends a leaf to `git-steward` for
the merge. No member widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that needs any of these
returns `blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`, and the conductor moves it to the human epic 1527.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the read-only opus correctness review reproduced every member against `main` 10263dd9, the ref this
  round starts from. That reproduction stands in for the scout.
- 1528 may only narrow: a read with no fetch (a cache or local-ref mode of `cq specs status`, or the HUD reading
  something already local), or an honest README and marketplace description. It never adds network, a hook, or a
  write.
- 1532 lands in the tool: a `cq specs epic` verb that moves (or removes) a membership. The autopilot protocol text
  (`commands/specs/autopilot.md`) stays as is, because changing its design is the human's call.
- 1529 may add a version-lockstep assertion for `plugins/quenching-hud/.claude-plugin/plugin.json`. That is a
  narrowing check, not a new executable surface.
- The session's agent definitions predate 1520's escalation change and 1519's verifier check 10. A reload of
  the plugins is the human's step, so this round runs with the definitions the session loaded. `cq` itself runs the
  checkout's current code.
