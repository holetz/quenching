## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 through 1596. After a verifier PASS, the run may execute 1600 forced, push, open the PR and
merge it into `main` with no further confirmation: the conductor sends the leaf to `git-steward` for the merge.
Nothing widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that needs one returns `blocked`
with `needs-human: alargamento`, and the conductor moves it to human epic 1527.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the opus review of epic 1596 reproduced 1600 against `main` a9217ed7, comparing it with a845eef0. The
  map was not run again, because every front was mapped in rounds 5, 9, 10 and 11, and everything that changed since
  has been reviewed.
- 1600: dependency and build folders are skipped only at the top level of the surface walk. The test walk follows
  pytest's default `norecursedirs`, without `vendor`. `setup.cfg [tool:pytest]` is read in pytest's order. Still no
  `.gitignore` reader and no subprocess in the proof front.
- Not taken as a spec: a quoted `pr: 'none'` in Azure. It is rare, and its effect is only to hide a warning.
