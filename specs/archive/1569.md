## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 through 1565. For every member, after a verifier PASS, the run may execute it forced, push,
open the PR and merge it into `main`, with no further confirmation, leaves included: the conductor sends leaves to
`git-steward` for the merge. No member widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that
needs one returns `blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`, and the conductor moves it to human epic 1527.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the opus correctness review of #1567 reproduced 1568 against `main` 793d8123. The map was not run
  again: since the map that found no candidate at 71f8f356, only `audit.py` and `cost.py` changed, and both were
  reviewed.
- 1568: a target counts as kept only when it is a `.py` the gate discovers. The discover command is read with
  comments ignored and quotes stripped. A form it does not recognize is reported as inconclusive, never passed in
  silence. Consolidating two tests into one stays flagged (one-to-one pairing, by design).
- 1564 is carried over from epic 1565 as `failed`. Its code is in worktree `~/Projetos/claude-quenching-1564`
  (local commit f6abf4ab, `--latest-orchestrator`, no `date` step, no new grant). What is left is the record: declare
  a task for the work and its files, and rewrite the Outcome to match the shipped design. Then verifier, push to
  #1566, merge. The `date -u` version of #1566 must never be merged.
