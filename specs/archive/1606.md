## Problem

Encontrado pelo mapa da rodada 15 do autopilot (2026-10-12) e confirmado pelo condutor em `main` a1f0344c.
`plugins/quenching/assets/references/proof-align/gate-contract.md:66` declara que "the gate contract owns these
codes and no other reference defines them", mas a tabela (`:68-…`) traz só os códigos do doctor. O
`cq proof ratchet` (`plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/proof/ratchet.py`) emite sete códigos, e só
`pf-no-floor` está documentado:
- `pf-coverage-missing` (`:138`)
- `pf-coverage-invalid` (`:148`)
- `pf-coverage-no-measured-root` (`:208`)
- `pf-floor-invalid` (`:219`)
- `pf-floor-regressed` (`:264`)
- `pf-floor-unmeasured` (`:274`, novo na #1584)

Desses, `pf-floor-regressed` e `pf-floor-unmeasured` são bloqueantes (`:279`): impedem o `--raise`. O
`plugins/quenching/commands/proof/align.md:99` chama `cq proof ratchet --raise --json` e roteia os achados pela banda
da tabela (`:71`), mas nenhum desses códigos tem banda.

Consequência: um agente no `/quenching:proof:align` que recebe `pf-floor-unmeasured` ou `pf-floor-regressed` no
`--raise` não tem instrução. Pode tomar o exit diferente de zero como ruído e relatar o piso como subido, ou tentar
"consertar" uma raiz que deixou de ser medida, o que é decisão do dono. A correção documenta os sete códigos na
tabela, com banda (a regressão e a raiz não medida são julgamento e vão ao humano; artefato ausente ou inválido é
recusa), e diz no `align.md` o que fazer quando o `--raise` é bloqueado.

## Proposal

- The finding-codes table in `gate-contract.md` lists every code `cq proof ratchet` emits, each with severity and band: `pf-floor-regressed` and `pf-floor-unmeasured` are judgement; `pf-coverage-missing`, `pf-coverage-invalid`, `pf-coverage-no-measured-root` and `pf-floor-invalid` are refusal.
- `proof/align.md` says that when `cq proof ratchet --raise` is blocked or refused, the run reports the code and root and never calls the floor raised.

## Out of Scope

- Any change to `ratchet.py` or its tests: prose only, the emitted codes are already correct.
- Changing `bands.md` or `docs/standards/architecture/proof-front.md`: the standard lists the gate-contract codes by name and is not part of this fix.
- Auto-repairing a regressed or unmeasured root: that is the owner's decision.

## Design

- Rule: the gate contract owns every `pf-*` code a proof command emits, and no code the ratchet emits is left without a band.
- Bands: a regressed floor or a root that stopped being measured is a judgement the owner takes (report to the human); a missing or invalid coverage artifact, a root-less configuration or an invalid floor file stops the ratchet with exit 2 and is a refusal. Alternative: calling the unmeasured root structural and dropping its floor was rejected, since it silently lowers the bar.
- Rule: a floor is raised only when `--raise` exits 0; any blocking finding or refusal leaves the floor file untouched, and the report says the floor was not raised.

## Alternatives Considered

- Document the codes only in `align.md`: rejected, the gate contract states it owns the codes and no other reference defines them.
