## Problem

A regra da 1464 que pula o hook `after_specs_execute_task` em worktree compara o texto de `git rev-parse --git-dir` com o de `--git-common-dir`. Num subdiretório do checkout principal, sem worktree nenhuma, os dois diferem só na forma (`/…/repo/.git` contra `../../.git`), e a regra lê "worktree" onde não há: se o run commita de um subdiretório, o `/security-review` nunca roda e sai `skipped: worktree`. Reprodução em `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-user-Projetos-claude-quenching/a284eb01-1fc3-4a7f-ae95-abf7651b3d1b/scratchpad/review20/h/` (`git -C repo/sub rev-parse --git-dir --git-common-dir`). Onde: `plugins/quenching/commands/specs/execute.md:298-299` e o espelho `plugins/quenching-codex/skills/quenching-specs-execute/SKILL.md:298`. Direção: comparar `git rev-parse --path-format=absolute --git-dir` com `--path-format=absolute --git-common-dir`.

Origem: revisão de correção final (opus, só leitura) do autopilot de 2026-10-08, sobre as PRs #1466–#1467 (épico 1463); por decisão do humano em 2026-10-09 o autopilot encerrou na rodada 11, e os achados ficam como specs soltas no backlog.

## Proposal

- The worktree rule in step 5g of `execute.md` compares absolute paths (`git rev-parse --path-format=absolute --git-dir` against `--path-format=absolute --git-common-dir`), so a commit made from a subdirectory of the main checkout is not classified as a worktree.
- The hook `after_specs_execute_task` runs in that subdirectory case and is still `skipped: worktree` in a real linked worktree.
- A test pins the absolute-path form in the command body.

## Out of Scope

- The Codex mirror (`plugins/quenching-codex/**`): retired, never touched.
- Changing which hooks are declared or their conditions: only the worktree detection wording moves.

## Design

A durable rule: a worktree is detected by comparing the **absolute** git-dir with the absolute git-common-dir, never their raw text. `--path-format=absolute` makes both resolve from any subdirectory, as `assets/bin/quenching/git/worktree.py` already does for the common dir.

## Alternatives Considered

- Comparing `--show-toplevel` against the common dir's parent: rejected, it breaks in bare or separate-git-dir layouts.
