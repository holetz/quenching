## Problem

`quenching/assets/bin/quenching/specs/commands/create.py:129-133`: the title is substituted into the template before the chained `.replace("<DATE>")` and `.replace("<VERIFICATION>")`, which then rewrite placeholders inside the title itself.

Reproduced in a temporary store (autopilot 2026-11-21 round 4, origin/main c29ba1e46): `cq specs new 'Placeholder <DATE> e <VERIFICATION>'` prints `created 'Placeholder <DATE> e <VERIFICATION>'`, but `cq specs list --lean --json` shows `"title": "Placeholder 2026-11-21 e per-section"`.

Consequence: the stored title (`title:` and the body `# `) differs from what the user passed while stdout claims otherwise; plausible in this repository, whose specs talk about the capture template. Fix direction: substitute in one pass (or the title last), with a test.

## Proposal

`cmd_new` (`quenching/assets/bin/quenching/specs/commands/create.py`) fills the capture template in one order that never rescans the title: `<DATE>` and `<VERIFICATION>` are substituted into the template first, and the title goes in last, through one single-pass `re.sub` over the two title placeholders (`title: <TITLE>` → the YAML scalar, `<TITLE>` → the raw title). A title carrying `<DATE>`, `<VERIFICATION>` or even `<TITLE>` is stored verbatim, so the stored `title:` and body `# ` equal what stdout reports. A regression test in `quenching/tests/test_specs_title.py` captures such a title through `cmd_new` and reads it back.

## Out of Scope

- none — the template text (`specs/schema.py`), the stdout message and the other `new` paths (tags, type, complexity, stream blocks) are untouched; only the substitution order changes.

## Design

- Substitution order: template-only placeholders (`<DATE>`, `<VERIFICATION>`) first, title last, in one `re.sub` pass with a callback over `title: <TITLE>|<TITLE>`, so the inserted title is never rescanned — not even for a literal `<TITLE>` inside it.
- The test sits in `TheCaptureStampsAReadableTitle` (same `_Workspace` fixture) and asserts both the frontmatter `title` and that the body carries `# <title>` verbatim.

### Assumptions

- The template's only placeholders are `<TITLE>`, `<DATE>` and `<VERIFICATION>` (`specs/schema.py:173-174`, `create.py:129-133`) — `## Design`, `## Tasks`.

## Alternatives Considered

- Only moving the title `.replace` calls after `<DATE>`/`<VERIFICATION>`: fixes the reported case but a literal `<TITLE>` inside the title still gets rescanned by the second `.replace("<TITLE>", …)` after `title: <TITLE>` was filled; rejected for the single-pass `re.sub`.
- Escaping the title before substitution and unescaping after: more moving parts for the same result; rejected.
