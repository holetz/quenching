## Problem

Os épicos 1192 e 1197 (2026-10-07/08, orquestração `--autonomous`, ~49 PRs mergeadas sem humano) mudaram superfície sensível: o `pr:create` passou a publicar sem confirmação sob `autonomous` (1185) e os agentes passaram a repassar o token (1185, 1188, 1205, 1219); o passo 5g do execute roda `git remote set-head origin -a` antes de um hook (1190) e o `/security-review` passou a mirar a worktree (1210); `translate --write` mudou o manifesto e a contenção de caminhos (1198, 1202); os grants de `git branch` do verifier e do orquestrador foram estreitados (1191, 1211); e o commit do execute passou a um verbo determinístico (1212). Na orquestração do 1192, o hook `/security-review` revisou o checkout base, com diff vazio, em 5 de 8 construções, e dois workers revisaram à mão; ou seja, a revisão declarada para essas mudanças não aconteceu de fato.

Consequência: mudanças em aprovação, publicação, hooks e escrita de arquivos entraram em `main` sem uma revisão de segurança efetiva, o mesmo buraco que as decisões 0008 e 0009 fecharam para os épicos anteriores.

## Proposal

Write decision `docs/decisions/0011-seguranca-das-mudancas-dos-epicos-1192-e-1197.md` in the shape of
decision 0009: one verdict per merged PR of epics 1192 and 1197 that touches sensitive surface (approval,
publication, credentials, hooks, file writes, grants), each read from its merge diff against the first
parent. Each real defect found is captured as its own spec with `cq specs new` (searching by title first,
so a retry never duplicates it), never fixed here. Index the decision in `docs/decisions/index.md` and in
the `zensical.toml` navigation.

## Out of Scope

- Fixing any finding: each real defect becomes its own spec through `cq specs new`, as the orchestrator
  asked; the decision only names it.
- Re-reviewing PRs decisions 0008 and 0009 already cover (#1159–#1162, #1172–#1184).
- PRs of the two epics with no sensitive surface (#1193, #1195, #1207, #1209, #1217, #1220, #1221,
  #1223, #1224, #1226): listed once in the decision as not sensitive, with no verdict section.
- Repairing the `/security-review` hook itself; its behaviour in the 1192 orchestration is recorded
  as evidence only.
