## Problem

Na orquestração `--autonomous` do épico 1163, o develop da spec 1166 gravou `priority.complexity: low` por engano, sobrescrevendo o `medium` que a spec trazia desde a criação, e o próprio worker teve de restaurá-lo (Discoveries da 1166: "develop stamped complexity=low by mistake and restored medium; the forced low gear should not touch the priority record"). O spec-runner pede ao develop o gear `low` **forçado** (`plugins/quenching/agents/spec-runner.md`, §How you work), e esse gear de execução é confundido com o registro de complexidade.

Consequência: a complexidade decide o modelo do worker e o roteamento do orquestrador (`agents/orchestrator.md`, §Route). Uma spec rebaixada para `low` passa a rodar com menos modelo e menos portão do que foi decidido.

## Proposal

- `develop.md` states, in its Doctrine and again in its Invariants, that the `low` gear a conductor declares as forced is the execution gear and never a level: it writes nothing to `priority`, and `complexity` on disk stays what it was.
- The only record a forced `low` pass adds is `approved` with `by=orchestrator-forced`, stamped after `ready` is proved; a `medium` spec leaves the pass still `medium`.
- `spec-runner.md` carries the same sentence beside the instruction that declares the forced gear, so the worker reads it where it acts.
- The Codex translation of `develop` is regenerated so both surfaces say the same.

## Out of Scope

- Changing how the orchestrator routes by `complexity` (`agents/orchestrator.md` §Route): the routing is correct, the record it reads was the one being clobbered.
- A tool-level guard in `cq specs record` that refuses a `priority.complexity` fall: ruled out here because the schema already lets `develop` write the field for legitimate raises, and a mechanical fall check is a separate spec.
- Restoring the 1166 record: the worker already restored it.

## Design

- **The forced declaration is a gear, not a level.** Doctrine already says "the gear is still `low`, and only the stamp at step 8 differs", but step 2 also defines the gear as `records.priority.complexity`, so a worker reading both can conclude that running at `low` means recording `low`. The fix states the separation once in the Doctrine and once as an Invariant, instead of rewording step 2, because step 2 is correct for the non-forced case where gear and level are the same value.
- **Rule as written:** a forced `low` pass writes nothing to `priority`; a level moves only through step 8's close, with a human.
- **Both surfaces get it.** The command body is what develop obeys; the spec-runner line is what the worker reads before it invokes develop. The Codex translation is generated, never hand-edited.
- Evidence: on main, `develop.md` has no sentence saying the forced declaration leaves `priority` untouched; its only guard (Invariants, "Never restamp `complexity` beyond what the close authorizes") did not prevent the spec 1166 incident.

### Mapa de dependências

- `plugins/quenching/commands/specs/develop.md` — source of the rule; translated into `plugins/quenching-codex/skills/quenching-specs-develop/SKILL.md` (regenerate or the translation check drifts).
- `plugins/quenching/agents/spec-runner.md` — cites the forced gear; read by `agents/orchestrator.md` only by reference.

## Alternatives Considered

- Reword step 2 so the gear is no longer `priority.complexity`: rejected, step 2's reading is correct in the common case and many references cite it.
- Guard in the `cq specs record` tool against any `complexity` fall: rejected for this spec (see Out of Scope); prose is where the ambiguity lives.

## Open Decisions

- Why now / what waiting costs: waiting repeats the 1166 error on every `--autonomous` epic and routes specs to a weaker model; decided by the human at triage, not by this pass.

## Risks

- The sentence is one more line in an already long body: ACCEPTED, it is one line in two places and replaces a worker-side restore.
- The translation drifts if regeneration is skipped: mitigated by task 2.1 and the repository gate.
