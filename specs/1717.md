## Problem

`cq knowledge project` splits a glossary term at the last em dash in its definition. Independent verification in `../resume-map-verify.json`, item 6, at e7bc280f: `- **Spec** — A unit of work — written in prose.` projects as `*[Spec — A unit of work]: written in prose.`; project check reports `OK — current` (exit 0).

Cause: `plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/knowledge/projection.py:101` uses `rsplit(" — ", 1)`. The glossary format in `assets/knowledge/glossary.md:17-18` does not prohibit em dashes inside definitions. Consequence: the intended tooltip disappears and an incorrect abbreviation is emitted with a green check. The current repository glossary has no affected entries; the template seed is already excluded by `_entry_from`, so impact is limited to consumers using this valid prose format.

Scope: separate the label from the definition at its actual delimiter and preserve the complete definition. Files: `plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/knowledge/projection.py` and `plugins/quenching/tests/test_knowledge_projection.py`. Cover literal em dashes inside bold/link labels so the repair does not merely move ambiguity. No template or format redesign. S5 precedes S8 conservatively.

Acceptance: the reproduction projects term `Spec` with its complete definition; bold/link labels containing em dashes remain correctly parsed; the new behavior check fails on the base. `python3 -m unittest discover -s plugins/quenching/tests -p test_knowledge_projection.py` and `bash scripts/verify_repo.sh` exit 0.
