## Problem

Human decision: `cq git stale` measures "merged" against the local base rather than the remote base. Scout map (`../resume-map-tools.json`, item 3, HEAD 719ca3e5): `plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/git/stale.py:20` and `:44` use `--merged <local base>`; `plugins/quenching/commands/git/cleanup.md:24-25` forbids fetch; `worktree retire` already uses `<remote>/<base>`. Measured in this checkout: `main` was 99 commits behind `origin/main`, with 12 branches merged into `origin/main` and only 1 into `main`.

Consequence: after merges through `gh`, the report undercounts merged branches, and branches/worktrees accumulate (the symptom of #1658).

Why this requires the human: changing the oracle changes what `prune` can delete. `branch -d` compares with the upstream or HEAD and would refuse the additional items, so this is a deletion-safety decision outside the autonomy policy.

Choose: (a) retain the local base; (b) measure against `<remote>/<base>` without fetch and align `branch -d`; (c) report both and delete only what both bases confirm. This spec only records the pending decision. No build, grant, hook or script change until the human chooses.
