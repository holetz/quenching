## Problem

Since #1505 (spec 1499), `TASK_META_RE` in `plugins/quenching/assets/bin/quenching/specs/parse/tasks.py:19` (and its copy
in `plugins/quenching-specs-reader`) accepts a `- ` bullet. Together with `re.IGNORECASE` and "last one wins" (lines
135-147), a prose bullet under a task overrides the real metadata. Reproduced: with `      verify: make t` plus
`  - Verify: run it by hand in the browser`, the base gives `verify: "make t"`, main gives `"run it by hand in the browser"`.
`  - Files: also touch whatever the linter needs` replaces `["a.ts"]`. Consequence: a note becomes the shell command of
the verify loop, and the real verification disappears; the real `files:` leaves the verifier's scope check (a false
FAIL). Latent today: only archive 1297 and 1470 use bullets, legitimately. The bare form must win over the bullet form,
and the bullet form must not be case-insensitive.
