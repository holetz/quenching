## Problem

O épico 1163 previa que o C08 (spec 1171) revisasse também os merges sensíveis das ondas 1 e 2. A decisão 0008 entregue cobre só #1159, #1160, #1161 e #1162 (`docs/decisions/0008-seguranca-das-mudancas-do-epico-1145.md:22-25`). O `## Outcome` da 1171 registra que "o hook de segurança por task não foi invocado", e nenhum `## Outcome` de 1166, 1168 ou 1170 menciona o hook. A spec 1167 (PR #1172) trocou o hook para `/security-review` com `condition`, mas nenhuma execução orquestrada mostrou até agora o hook disparando.

Merges sensíveis do épico 1163 sem revisão: #1172 (config do hook, formato de retorno do spec-runner e leitura do orchestrator), #1175 (Forbidden e tools do spec-runner e do verifier), #1173 (retry de escrita no backend GitHub), #1177 (guardas de listagem GitHub e Azure), #1174 (functional-checks) e os PRs das specs 1165, 1169 e dos follow-ups 09 e 10.

Consequência: a pergunta original do humano ("o hook não rodou em nenhuma das specs") continua sem resposta medida; superfície publicada em `main` sem revisão.

## Proposal

- A decision `0009` records a verdict per merged PR of epic 1163 that touches a sensitive surface (#1172, #1173, #1174, #1175, #1177, #1181, #1182, #1183, #1184), each read from its merge diff.
- The decision carries measured evidence about the `after_specs_execute_task` hook: either a `Skill` invocation of `security-review` made from a worker context and its `ran|failed|unresolved` classification, or the measured reason it does not fire.
- The decision is indexed in `docs/decisions/index.md` and in the `zensical.toml` navigation.

## Out of Scope

- Fixing any finding: a finding is one `## Discoveries` line, as in decision 0008.
- Re-reviewing #1159, #1160, #1161 and #1162: decision 0008 already covers them.
- Re-running the full orchestrated flow (`/quenching:orchestrate`) to watch the hook: the evidence is one `Skill` call from a worker context, which is the claim in dispute.

## Validation

- Uma decisão (ou adendo à 0008) com veredito e evidência por merge listado acima.
- Evidência de uma execução de task em que o hook `after_specs_execute_task` foi invocado (tool_use Skill `security-review` num capture ou a linha `HOOKS:` do retorno de um worker), ou o motivo medido de ele não disparar, convertido em spec.

## Design

Durable rule: a merge to the primary branch that touches a workflow, release, approval stamp, hook, script or an agent's tool grant gets a read-only verdict recorded in `docs/decisions/` when the per-task hook did not run for it. Format follows decision 0008 (table, then one section per PR, findings parked as discoveries).

Verdicts, read from the merge diffs (evidence gathered while composing, assumption: the merge diff is the whole change):

| PR | Surface | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #1172 | `.claude/quenching.json`, `.agents/quenching.json`, `execute.md`, `spec-runner.md` | Accepted. The hook is now `/security-review`, `optional`, with a free-text `condition` the executor passes as context and never evaluates; no command, grant or permission was added. `HOOKS:` only adds a report line. |

Hook evidence: the spec-runner declares the `Skill` tool, and `/security-review` is a built-in skill. The proof is to invoke `Skill("security-review", ...)` from this worker, with the `condition` text as context, over the task that writes the decision, and classify the outcome as `ran`, `failed` or `unresolved`. The task commit is the evidence anchor. If the call does not resolve or run, the measured reason is parked as a discovery for conclude to turn into a spec.

## Alternatives Considered

- An addendum to decision 0008: rejected, a decision is append-only and 0008 is scoped to epic 1145 by its title and filename.
- Running the whole orchestrator to observe the hook in its natural place: rejected as out of proportion for a low-complexity chore; the worker-context `Skill` call isolates the disputed fact (does the hook resolve and run from a sub-agent).

## Open Decisions

- Whether the `condition` text of the hook should be evaluated by the executor instead of passed as context: not decided here; decided by a spec if the hook evidence shows the condition is ignored or misread.

## Risks

- A worker that invokes the hook once proves it can fire, not that it fires on every sensitive task: ACCEPTED — the decision states exactly what was measured and no more.
- The verdicts rest on the merge diffs read now; a later commit can change them: ACCEPTED — the decision is dated and append-only.
