## Problem

The plugin's context limits are unmeasured numbers ("under 150k… a worker under 200k",
`agents/orchestrator.md` step 3), while the standard prohibits any threshold for a human-led
session (`context-discipline.md` §Run for less time: "No threshold, no token count").

Measured on 2026-10-13 across 1,383 transcripts (`input + cache_read + cache_creation` per
`message.id`): the 57 main sessions have median peak 104k, p90 292k, maximum 510k; 14 (25%)
exceed 200k and account for 86% of the 599M-token integral. Across 1,326 subagents the median
is 20k, p90 85k, maximum 244k, with 5 above 200k. The worst command runs are `autopilot`
451k/293 turns, `execute` 370k/185, `improve` 274k/140, and `orchestrate` 247k/109.

What is missing to turn that evidence into a rule is (a) the distribution by command and
stage (`develop`, `execute`, `conclude`, conductor, worker), with honest attribution under
`session-evidence.md`; (b) the resumption cost of a fresh context, the cold read declared but
not measured by `context-discipline.md` §What a delegated executor costs, which is the floor
below which fragmentation costs more than it saves; and (c) the three budgets (main session,
worker, conductor) derived from those two curves, and the target reduction from 86%.

The deliverable is the measured table and the three numbers, recorded with sample and date
in `context-discipline.md` in place of the threshold prohibition. S3–S6 read those numbers;
none invents its own.

## Proposal

- Publish a reproducible, sanitized distribution of observed context windows and token exposure
  by main session, worker and conductor, and by attributed command/stage, with sample date.
- Measure fresh-context entry windows and cold input separately from incremental resumption
  cost; make missing or ambiguous evidence explicit.
- Replace the threshold prohibition with three evidence-derived budgets and a stated reduction
  target once restart evidence and the target selection criterion are sufficient.

## Out of Scope

- The live-session context verb and enforcement in command bodies or agents: S3–S5 own them.
- Build-cost estimation and decomposition: S6 consumes the resulting budgets.
- Pricing inference from token exposure, or conversion between model tiers or host models.
- Publishing transcript bodies, prompts, tool payloads or sensitive absolute machine paths.
- Paid fresh-session experiments without an experiment design and authorized cost ceiling.

## Design

Count distinct assistant `message.id`, keeping final usage by largest `output_tokens`. The
captured-baseline metric sums `input + cache_read + cache_creation` once per request; peak is
its maximum and integral is its sum. Top-level usage may sum executor iterations. Publish a
separate per-executor-iteration context peak and coverage; never confuse cumulative request
exposure with one context window or include advisor-model usage as executor usage. This is
context/token exposure, not money or remaining host capacity.

Use existing `close_attribution` to identify typed and Skill entry forms and unclosed conducted
stages. An unclosed stage's pointer-attributed counts are upper bounds; unknown attribution
stays unknown. A stage distribution covers attributed requests, not every turn in the session
that invoked it. Main/subagent topology and conductor/worker metadata are independent axes.

The first observed request remains an entry proxy. Operational resumption preparation is
measured separately: explicit worker Resume directives paired with a preceding task-check
invocation for the same spec, bounded through the first `cq specs next` invocation. State the
small opus-only sample and do not infer task-check success, warm-counterfactual savings or
clean main/conductor resumption cost. The observed preparation peak is a worker floor, not a
universal assertion about every role.

Select budgets as an engineering policy derived from the role distributions and observed
worker preparation: main `200000`, worker `100000`, conductor `150000`. The main cut isolates
14/58 usage-bearing sessions carrying 85.95% of main exposure, with fewer historical sessions
affected than 150k (23/58). Worker 100k rounds above its p90 97126 and exceeds the largest
observed preparation peak 33159 threefold; conductor 150k rounds above its p90 127383 and
historically affects 3/49. These are selected policy values, not quantities the transcripts
measure automatically. No level or host-model mapping changes.

Use the observed worker share of exposure from sessions above 200k (4.324947%) as a reference
for a main-session target of at most `4.33%` (`0.0433`, rounded upward). It is a project
hypothesis about the distribution after S3–S6 establish context boundaries, not a simulation
of measured savings, a causal prediction or a claim that the roles have identical workloads.
Model mix, scope, attribution coverage, restart evidence and boundary overshoot must be
reported in field validation; this spike does not fabricate that future outcome.

Budget checks fire only at existing stage ends, task-section ends and worker returns, never
in the middle of a task. The standard owns the numeric policy; S3–S6 own instrumentation,
enforcement, handoff and later verification. Authority remains background until the existing
second-adopting-repository graduation condition is actually met.

## Alternatives Considered

- Copy the current 150k/200k prose into the standard: rejected because its absence of measurement
  is precisely the captured defect.
- Treat first-request context as restart overhead: rejected because it includes resident
  harness/input and proves no counterfactual cost of the next task.
- Attribute every later turn to the last invoked Skill as an exact span: rejected by the
  session-evidence contract and measured unclosed-pointer behavior.
- Run paid paired sessions immediately: deferred until restart experiment/cost is authorized;
  use the existing transcript corpus first.

## Open Decisions

- Clean main/conductor resumption preparation is not measured: collect identified role-specific
  pairs in S3–S6 field validation rather than silently extrapolating the four worker pairs.
- The 4.33% target is a hypothesis to test after enforcement, with model mix, attribution
  coverage and workload differences reported. Observed causal savings remain unmeasured.
## Risks

- Split content blocks inflate counts: fixtures prove one request and final usage.
- Attribution bleeds into a caller: closed status is carried into each command distribution.
- Local corpus changes or records are incomplete: record inventory hash, sample instant,
  anomalies, missing usage and added transcripts; never call an inconclusive read complete.
- Entry proxy is mistaken for causal savings: name it explicitly and leave incremental
  restart cost unmeasured until supported.
- ACCEPTED — this internal sample is not a second adopting repo; standard authority stays
  background even if the local counts and repository gate pass.
