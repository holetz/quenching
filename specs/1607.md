## Problem

O mapa da rodada 15 do autopilot de 2026-10-11 encontrou deriva de prosa depois dos merges do dia.
O gate-contract de proof não define os códigos do ratchet e o align não diz o que fazer quando o `--raise` é bloqueado.

## Design

### Autonomy policy

Authorized on 2026-10-11 by the human's invocation of `/quenching:specs:autopilot until dry`, the same one that
authorized epics 1522 through 1604. After a verifier PASS, the run may execute 1606 forced, push, open the PR and
merge it into `main` with no further confirmation: the conductor sends the leaf to `git-steward` for the merge.
Nothing widens a grant, a hook, CI, or a surface that executes code. A worker that needs any of these returns
`blocked` with `needs-human: alargamento`.

### Conductor decisions (as the delegated owner)

- Relevance: the round-15 map found it, and the conductor confirmed it against `ratchet.py:138-279` and
  `gate-contract.md:66`.
- 1606 is prose only. The table in `gate-contract.md` gains the seven codes the ratchet emits, with their bands:
  `pf-floor-regressed` and `pf-floor-unmeasured` are judgement and go to the human, and a missing or invalid artifact
  or floor file is a refusal. `proof/align.md` says what to do when `--raise` is blocked: report it, never call the
  floor raised. No code changes.
- Not taken as a spec: the command `cleanup.md` displays differs from the leased delete. Its refusals are already
  reported generically, and the command that actually runs is the safer one.
